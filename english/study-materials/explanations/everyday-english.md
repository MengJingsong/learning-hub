# Everyday English Explanations

Expressions are explained in common North American usage. Similar expressions may differ in tone or context.

## Core

### Fingers crossed
**词汇补充：** /ˈfɪŋɡərz ˈkrɔst/；常搭配 *keep your fingers crossed, fingers crossed that...*；近义 *hope for the best*；无常用反义词。
**English explanation:** An informal way to express hope that something will go well.
**意思：** 但愿好运、希望顺利；常伴随等待结果。类似：*I hope it works out.*
1. Fingers crossed the interview goes well. — 希望面试顺利。
2. We're waiting for the test results—fingers crossed. — 我们在等检测结果，希望一切顺利。

### You bet
**意思：** 当然、没问题；也可表示“你说得对”。类似：*Absolutely; sure.*
**English explanation:** A casual way to say yes, certainly, or you're welcome, depending on context.
1. “Can you send it today?” “You bet.” — “你今天能发吗？”“当然可以。”
2. “Was it difficult?” “You bet.” — “难吗？”“那当然。”

### I'm all ears
**意思：** 我洗耳恭听，表示愿意认真听。类似：*I'm listening; go ahead.*
**English explanation:** I'm ready and willing to listen carefully.
1. You said you found a better approach—I'm all ears. — 你说找到更好的方法了，我洗耳恭听。
2. I'm all ears if you have feedback. — 如果你有反馈，我愿意认真听。

### What's the catch?
**意思：** 有什么隐情/条件/代价吗？常用于看似很好的提议。类似：*What's the downside?*
**English explanation:** What hidden condition, disadvantage, or cost comes with this offer?
1. The software is free. What's the catch? — 软件免费？有什么条件吗？
2. They offered a much lower price, but I wonder what's the catch. — 他们给了更低的价格，但我想知道有什么隐情。

### Out of the blue
**意思：** 突然、出乎意料地。类似：*unexpectedly; all of a sudden.*
**English explanation:** Suddenly and unexpectedly.
1. He called me out of the blue. — 他突然给我打电话。
2. The idea came to her out of the blue. — 她突然想到了这个主意。

### It's not rocket science
**意思：** 这并不复杂/难懂；口语，有时略显不耐烦。类似：*It's not that complicated.*
**English explanation:** It's not very difficult to understand or do; the phrase can sound impatient.
1. The setup is simple; it's not rocket science. — 设置很简单，没那么复杂。
2. It's not rocket science—you just need to follow the steps. — 这不难，照步骤做就行。

### Cut to the chase
**意思：** 直奔主题、别绕弯子。类似：*get to the point.*
**English explanation:** Get to the main point without spending time on less important details.
1. Let's cut to the chase: what is the cost? — 我们直奔主题吧：费用是多少？
2. I'll cut to the chase and explain the risk. — 我就不绕弯子了，直接说明风险。

### You're spot on
**意思：** 你说得完全正确/很到位。类似：*Exactly; you're absolutely right.*
**English explanation:** You are exactly right.
1. You're spot on about the main bottleneck. — 你对主要瓶颈的判断完全正确。
2. That description is spot on. — 这个描述非常准确。

### It's a tough call
**意思：** 这是个难以抉择的决定。类似：*It's a hard decision.*
**English explanation:** It's a difficult decision because the choices are hard to compare.
1. Choosing between reliability and cost is a tough call. — 在可靠性和成本之间选择很难。
2. It's a tough call, so let's compare the evidence. — 这很难决定，我们来比较一下证据。

### That's way over my head
**意思：** 那超出我的理解范围；way 加强语气。类似：*That's beyond my understanding.*
**English explanation:** That's too difficult or technical for me to understand.
1. The proof is way over my head. — 这个证明我完全看不懂。
2. The financial details are over my head. — 财务细节超出了我的理解范围。

### In the loop
**意思：** 知情并持续收到进展。类似：*kept informed; up to date.*
**English explanation:** Informed about what is happening and receiving updates.
1. Keep me in the loop about the schedule. — 日程有变化就告诉我。
2. The support team is in the loop now. — 支持团队现在也了解情况了。

### Put it on hold
**意思：** 暂停或搁置某事；也可指让电话等待。类似：*pause; put it on pause.*
**English explanation:** Pause or postpone it; on a phone call, keep the other person waiting temporarily.
1. We put the redesign on hold until funding is approved. — 资金获批前，我们搁置了重设计。
2. Could you put me on hold for a moment? — 你能让我稍等一下吗？

### Sleep on it
**意思：** 暂不决定，睡一觉后再考虑。类似：*think it over; decide later.*
**English explanation:** Wait until later, often until the next day, before making a decision.
1. Let me sleep on it before I accept. — 让我考虑一晚再答应。
2. It's a big decision; you can sleep on it. — 这是个重大决定，你可以明天再做决定。

### Push back on
**意思：** 对某个提议/要求提出异议或抵制。类似：*challenge; object to.*
**English explanation:** To object to or challenge a proposal, demand, or decision.
1. She pushed back on the proposed deadline. — 她对拟定的截止日期提出了异议。
2. We should push back on unrealistic assumptions. — 我们应该反对不切实际的假设。

### Heads-up
**词汇补充：** /ˈhɛdzˌʌp/；常搭配 *give someone a heads-up, just a heads-up*；近义 *warning, advance notice*；反义 *surprise, no notice*。
**English explanation:** A brief advance warning or useful notice.
**意思：** 预先提醒、提示（名词）。类似：*advance warning; alert.*
1. Thanks for the heads-up about the outage. — 谢谢你提前提醒我服务中断。
2. Just a heads-up: the room has changed. — 提醒一下：会议室换了。

### I'm not following
**意思：** 我没听懂/没跟上你的意思。类似：*I don't understand; I lost you.*
**English explanation:** I don't understand what you mean or have lost track of the explanation.
1. I'm not following the last step. — 最后一步我没听懂。
2. Sorry, I'm not following—could you explain the link? — 抱歉我没跟上，你能解释一下这个关联吗？

### jump in
**意思：** 加入谈话/活动；打断并发言。类似：*join in; chime in.*
**English explanation:** To join an activity or conversation, sometimes by speaking before someone else has finished.
1. Feel free to jump in with questions. — 随时可以插进来提问。
2. She jumped in to help with the demo. — 她主动加入帮忙做演示。

### report back
**意思：** 之后汇报结果或进展。类似：*get back to someone with an update.*
**English explanation:** To return later with information, results, or an update.
1. I'll check the logs and report back. — 我会查日志再向你汇报。
2. Please report back by Friday. — 请在周五前反馈结果。

### check in
**意思：** 联系确认情况；报到；入住。类似：*touch base; confirm.*
**English explanation:** To contact someone to see how things are going; it can also mean register or arrive.
1. I'll check in tomorrow to see how it went. — 我明天联系你，看看进展如何。
2. We checked in at the hotel after lunch. — 午饭后我们在酒店办理了入住。

### keep someone posted
**意思：** 持续向某人更新消息。类似：*keep someone informed.*
**English explanation:** To continue giving someone the latest information.
1. Keep me posted on the review. — 审查有进展就告诉我。
2. I'll keep you posted if the plan changes. — 计划有变化我会及时通知你。

### touch base
**意思：** 简短联系、沟通一下。类似：*check in; connect briefly.*
**English explanation:** To make brief contact or have a short conversation.
1. Let's touch base after the meeting. — 我们会后简单沟通一下。
2. I wanted to touch base about the draft. — 我想就草稿跟你沟通一下。

### sync up
**意思：** 对齐信息/安排，或同步数据。类似：*coordinate; get on the same page.*
**English explanation:** To coordinate plans or make sure information is aligned; also, to synchronize data.
1. Let's sync up before we meet the client. — 见客户前我们先对一下信息。
2. The devices sync up automatically. — 这些设备会自动同步。

### get someone up to speed
**意思：** 让某人快速了解情况。类似：*bring someone up to date; brief someone.*
**English explanation:** To give someone the information needed to understand the current situation.
1. Can you get me up to speed on the project? — 你能让我快速了解项目进展吗？
2. I'll get the new student up to speed. — 我会让新同学尽快熟悉情况。

### on the same page
**意思：** 达成共识、对情况理解一致。类似：*in agreement; aligned.*
**English explanation:** Sharing the same understanding or agreement about something.
1. Let's make sure we're on the same page about the goals. — 我们确认一下对目标的理解是否一致。
2. The teams are finally on the same page. — 各团队终于达成一致了。

### loop someone in
**意思：** 把某人加入讨论/邮件，让其知情。类似：*include someone; bring someone into the conversation.*
**English explanation:** To include someone in a conversation or update them about an issue.
1. I'll loop Priya in on the thread. — 我会把 Priya 加入邮件讨论。
2. Loop me in when you contact the vendor. — 联系供应商时把我也抄送进来。

### play it by ear
**意思：** 到时视情况而定、随机应变。类似：*decide as you go; stay flexible.*
**English explanation:** To decide what to do as a situation develops instead of making a fixed plan in advance.
1. We don't need a strict schedule; let's play it by ear. — 不用定严格日程，到时看情况吧。
2. If the weather changes, we'll play it by ear. — 天气有变化的话我们就随机应变。

### gain traction
**意思：** 开始获得关注、支持或进展。类似：*catch on; gain momentum.*
**English explanation:** To begin attracting support, attention, or measurable progress.
1. The proposal gained traction after the demo. — 演示后，这个提案开始得到支持。
2. The product is gaining traction with researchers. — 这个产品正逐渐受到研究人员欢迎。

### hit a roadblock
**意思：** 遇到障碍/进展受阻。类似：*run into an obstacle; get stuck.*
**English explanation:** To encounter an obstacle that stops or slows progress.
1. We hit a roadblock when the API changed. — API 更改时我们遇到了障碍。
2. The project hit a roadblock over data access. — 项目在数据访问方面受阻。

### get back on track
**意思：** 恢复正常进度/回到正轨。类似：*resume progress; get back on schedule.*
**English explanation:** To return to the planned schedule or direction after a delay or problem.
1. We need a smaller milestone to get back on track. — 我们需要设一个更小的里程碑来恢复进度。
2. After the delay, the team got back on track. — 延误后团队重新回到了正轨。

### keep an eye on
**意思：** 留意、持续观察。类似：*watch; monitor.*
**English explanation:** To watch or monitor something carefully.
1. Keep an eye on the error rate. — 留意错误率。
2. I'll keep an eye on the kids while you call. — 你打电话时我会照看孩子。

### up in the air
**意思：** 尚未决定/不确定。类似：*undecided; unsettled.*
**English explanation:** Not yet settled or decided.
1. The launch date is still up in the air. — 发布日期仍未确定。
2. Our travel plans are up in the air. — 我们的旅行计划还没定。

### set in stone
**意思：** 已定、不可更改，常用否定式表示仍可调整。类似：*fixed; unchangeable.*
**English explanation:** Fixed and unlikely or impossible to change.
1. The schedule isn't set in stone yet. — 日程还不是完全定死的。
2. Once the contract is signed, the terms are set in stone. — 合同签署后条款就确定了。

### take ownership of
**意思：** 主动承担责任并跟进到底。类似：*take responsibility for.*
**English explanation:** To accept responsibility for handling something and its outcome.
1. She took ownership of the deployment issue. — 她主动负责跟进部署问题。
2. We need someone to take ownership of the documentation. — 我们需要有人负责文档工作。

### hand off
**意思：** 把任务/责任/控制权交给别人。类似：*pass on; transfer responsibility.*
**English explanation:** To transfer a task, responsibility, or control to someone else.
1. I'll hand off the ticket to the support team. — 我会把工单转给支持团队。
2. The service hands off the request to a worker. — 服务会把请求交给一个工作进程。

### on track
**意思：** 按计划进行；朝正确方向发展。类似：*on schedule; progressing as planned.*
**English explanation:** Progressing according to the plan or toward the intended goal.
1. We're on track to finish by Friday. — 我们有望按计划在周五前完成。
2. The experiment is on track so far. — 到目前为止实验进展正常。

### fall behind
**意思：** 落后于计划/进度或他人。类似：*lag behind; get behind.*
**English explanation:** To make less progress than planned or than other people.
1. We fell behind schedule after the outage. — 服务中断后我们进度落后了。
2. She fell behind on her reading. — 她的阅读进度落后了。

### raise a red flag
**意思：** 发出警讯，表示可能有问题。类似：*signal a warning; cause concern.*
**English explanation:** To signal a possible problem or reason for concern.
1. The sudden spike raised a red flag. — 突然的峰值发出了警讯。
2. That clause raises a red flag for the legal team. — 那条款让法务团队觉得有风险。

### take a stab at
**意思：** 试着做某事/给出答案，语气随意。类似：*give it a try; attempt.*
**English explanation:** To try doing or answering something, often without being certain of success.
1. I'll take a stab at fixing the script. — 我试着修一下脚本。
2. Can you take a stab at estimating the cost? — 你能试着估算一下成本吗？

### give it a shot
**意思：** 试试看。类似：*give it a try; have a go.*
**English explanation:** To try something and see whether it works.
1. I haven't used this tool, but I'll give it a shot. — 我没用过这个工具，不过我试试。
2. Give it a shot before ruling it out. — 排除它之前先试试看。

### get a handle on
**意思：** 理解/掌握/控制某事。类似：*get a grasp of; get control of.*
**English explanation:** To understand, manage, or gain control of something.
1. We need to get a handle on the memory growth. — 我们得弄清楚并控制内存增长。
2. It took me a week to get a handle on the codebase. — 我花了一周才摸清代码库。

### get a sense of
**意思：** 大致了解、形成印象。类似：*get an idea of; gauge.*
**English explanation:** To form a general understanding or impression of something.
1. The pilot will help us get a sense of demand. — 试点能帮助我们大致了解需求。
2. I read the abstract to get a sense of the paper. — 我读摘要来了解论文大意。

### have a rough idea of
**意思：** 对某事有大致概念，但不精确。类似：*have a general idea of.*
**English explanation:** To know something approximately, without precise details.
1. I have a rough idea of how the system works. — 我大致知道系统如何运作。
2. Do you have a rough idea of the timeline? — 你对时间安排有大致概念吗？

### Sounds good.
**意思：** 听起来不错/可以。类似：*That works; great.*
**English explanation:** That seems acceptable or like a good plan to me.
1. “Let's meet at two.” “Sounds good.” — “我们两点见。”“好。”
2. Sounds good to me; send the invite. — 我觉得可以，把邀请发来吧。

### That works for me.
**意思：** 对我来说可以/合适。类似：*That suits me; I'm fine with that.*
**English explanation:** That arrangement is suitable or acceptable for me.
1. Friday morning works for me. — 周五上午我可以。
2. “Can we move it to noon?” “That works for me.” — “能改到中午吗？”“我可以。”

### I'm all set.
**意思：** 我准备好了/不需要更多帮助；语境决定。类似：*I'm ready; I don't need anything else.*
**English explanation:** I'm ready, or I don't need anything more, depending on context.
1. I'm all set for the presentation. — 演示准备好了。
2. “Would you like a bag?” “I'm all set, thanks.” — “需要袋子吗？”“不用了，谢谢。”

### No worries.
**意思：** 没关系/别担心；轻松友好。类似：*No problem; don't worry about it.*
**English explanation:** That's all right; don't be concerned about it.
1. “Sorry I'm late.” “No worries.” — “抱歉我迟到了。”“没关系。”
2. No worries if you need another day. — 如果你还需要一天也没关系。

### I'm good.
**意思：** 我没事/不用了/我很好，依语境。类似：*I'm fine; no thanks.*
**English explanation:** I'm fine, or I don't need anything, depending on context.
1. “Do you need help?” “I'm good, thanks.” — “需要帮忙吗？”“不用了，谢谢。”
2. I'm good with either option. — 两种选择我都可以。

### Fair enough.
**意思：** 有道理/可以理解，表示接受对方理由。类似：*That makes sense; reasonable.*
**English explanation:** I understand and accept that point as reasonable.
1. “I need more time.” “Fair enough.” — “我需要更多时间。”“可以理解。”
2. Fair enough; let's revisit it next week. — 有道理，我们下周再讨论。

### Makes sense.
**意思：** 说得通/我理解了。类似：*That is logical; I understand.*
**English explanation:** That is logical or understandable.
1. Your explanation makes sense now. — 你的解释现在说得通了。
2. Makes sense—we should test the smaller case first. — 有道理，我们应该先测试较小的情况。

### Go ahead.
**意思：** 请继续/可以开始；也可表示许可。类似：*Please proceed; feel free.*
**English explanation:** You may begin, continue, or do what you asked permission to do.
1. Go ahead and share your screen. — 请共享屏幕吧。
2. “Can I ask a question?” “Go ahead.” — “我能问个问题吗？”“请问。”

### No rush.
**意思：** 不着急/不用赶。类似：*Take your time; there's no hurry.*
**English explanation:** There is no need to hurry; take the time you need.
1. No rush—send it when you're ready. — 不着急，准备好再发。
2. There's no rush to decide today. — 今天不必急着决定。

### Give me a sec.
**意思：** 等我一下，口语表达。类似：*Give me a moment; one second.*
**English explanation:** Wait briefly while I do something.
1. Give me a sec; I'm opening the file. — 等我一下，我正在打开文件。
2. Can you give me a sec to check? — 能等我一下让我查查吗？

### I'll get back to you.
**意思：** 我之后回复你/给你答复。类似：*I'll follow up; I'll let you know.*
**English explanation:** I will contact you later with a response or decision.
1. I'll check with the team and get back to you. — 我问问团队再回复你。
2. I don't know yet, but I'll get back to you tomorrow. — 我现在还不知道，明天答复你。

### I'm not sure I follow.
**意思：** 我不太明白/没跟上。比直接说“不懂”更委婉。类似：*Could you clarify?*
**English explanation:** I may not understand; this is a polite way to ask for clarification.
1. I'm not sure I follow the last assumption. — 最后一个假设我不太明白。
2. I'm not sure I follow—what does the arrow represent? — 我不太明白，这个箭头表示什么？

### pretty much
**意思：** 几乎；差不多；大体上。类似：*almost; more or less.*
**English explanation:** Almost or nearly; also, generally or in most important respects.
1. The setup is pretty much complete. — 设置基本完成了。
2. That's pretty much what I meant. — 那差不多就是我的意思。

### kind of
**意思：** 有点、某种程度上；非正式缓和语气。类似：*somewhat; rather.*
**English explanation:** Somewhat or to a limited degree; informally, it can soften a statement.
1. I'm kind of tired after the trip. — 旅行后我有点累。
2. The result is kind of surprising. — 这个结果有点出人意料。

### sort of
**意思：** 有点、算是；非正式缓和语气，常可与 kind of 互换。类似：*somewhat; kind of.*
**English explanation:** Somewhat or approximately; an informal expression often similar to kind of.
1. I sort of understand the idea. — 我大概理解这个想法。
2. It was sort of an experiment. — 那算是一次实验。

### I mean
**意思：** 用于解释、改述或填充停顿；频繁使用会显得犹豫。类似：*that is; what I'm saying is.*
**English explanation:** An informal phrase used to clarify or restate an idea, or to fill a pause.
1. I mean, we can try the simpler option first. — 我的意思是，我们可以先试试更简单的方案。
2. It was useful—I mean, especially for new students. — 它很有用，我是说，对新生尤其有用。

### check back soon
**意思：** 过一会儿再查看/联系。类似：*come back later; follow up soon.*
**English explanation:** Return or contact someone again after a short time to get an update.
1. The page is updating; check back soon. — 页面正在更新，请过一会儿再来看。
2. I'll check back soon to see whether it's ready. — 我很快再确认它是否准备好了。

### Way to go!
**意思：** 做得好！也可讽刺地表示“真行啊”。类似：*Great job!*
**English explanation:** An expression of praise for someone's success; tone can make it sarcastic.
1. You fixed it—way to go! — 你修好了，干得漂亮！
2. Way to go, team; we finished a day early. — 干得好，团队提前一天完成了。

### hard to follow
**意思：** 难以理解/跟上。类似：*difficult to understand; unclear.*
**English explanation:** Difficult to understand or keep up with.
1. The speaker was hard to follow. — 这位演讲者的话很难听懂。
2. The proof is hard to follow without a diagram. — 没有图示，这个证明很难跟上。

### make X up
**意思：** 编造 X；也可补足/弥补。类似：*invent X; compensate for X.*
**English explanation:** To invent X; in other contexts, to compensate for or complete something.
1. He made up an excuse for missing class. — 他为缺课编了个借口。
2. We can make up the lost time tomorrow. — 我们明天可以把损失的时间补回来。

### off the top of my head
**意思：** 不经查找、凭第一印象/记忆。类似：*from memory; without checking.*
**English explanation:** From memory or without checking carefully first.
1. Off the top of my head, I think there are three options. — 我一时想到的有三个选项。
2. I can't recall the exact figure off the top of my head. — 我现在想不起确切数字。

## Useful

### Screw up
**意思：** 搞砸/犯错；粗俗程度较轻但非正式。类似：*mess up; make a mistake.*
**English explanation:** To make a mistake or handle something badly; informal and mildly vulgar.
1. I screwed up the first calculation. — 我第一次计算搞错了。
2. Don't worry—we can fix what we screwed up. — 别担心，我们能修正搞砸的部分。

### Tell me about it
**意思：** “可不是嘛/我太懂了”，表示强烈认同，通常不是在索要信息。类似：*I know exactly what you mean.*
**English explanation:** An informal way to strongly agree that something is difficult or familiar, not usually a request for details.
1. “This week is exhausting.” “Tell me about it.” — “这周累坏了。”“可不是嘛。”
2. Tell me about it—the queue has been backed up all morning. — 可不是嘛，队列整个上午都堵着。

### Just my luck
**意思：** 真倒霉，常带无奈或反讽。类似：*What bad luck; typical.*
**English explanation:** An expression of frustration about an unlucky event, often with irony.
1. The bus left just as I arrived—just my luck. — 我刚到公交车就开走了，真倒霉。
2. Just my luck, the printer stopped working before the deadline. — 真倒霉，截止日期前打印机坏了。

### Jump the gun
**意思：** 操之过急、过早行动。类似：*act prematurely; get ahead of yourself.*
**English explanation:** To act too early, before the right time or before all information is available.
1. We jumped the gun by announcing the result. — 我们过早宣布了结果。
2. Don't jump the gun; wait for the approval. — 别操之过急，等批准下来。

### Hit the nail on the head
**意思：** 说到点子上/准确指出关键。类似：*be exactly right; pinpoint it.*
**English explanation:** To identify the exact point or describe something accurately.
1. You hit the nail on the head—the cache is the bottleneck. — 你说到点子上了，瓶颈就是缓存。
2. Her comment hit the nail on the head. — 她的评论准确指出了关键。

### I'm out of words
**意思：** 我不知道说什么/无话可说；可能是震惊、感动或无奈。类似：*I'm speechless.*
**English explanation:** I don't know what to say, often because I'm surprised, moved, or frustrated.
1. I'm out of words after seeing the result. — 看到结果后我都不知道说什么了。
2. I'm out of words—thank you for all your help. — 我不知道该说什么来表达感谢，谢谢你帮了这么多。

### Not in the loop
**意思：** 不知情/没有收到进展信息。类似：*out of the loop; uninformed.*
**English explanation:** Not informed about current events or updates.
1. I was not in the loop about the schedule change. — 我不知道日程变更的事。
2. Keep her included so she isn't left out of the loop. — 把她也加进来，免得她不知情。

### Pull one's weight
**意思：** 尽自己应尽的一份力。类似：*do one's share; contribute fairly.*
**English explanation:** To do a fair share of the work or responsibility in a group.
1. Everyone needs to pull their weight on this project. — 这个项目里每个人都要尽自己的责任。
2. He pulled his weight during the busy week. — 忙碌的一周里他尽了自己的一份力。

### Do one's fair share
**意思：** 公平地承担自己应做的部分。类似：*pull one's weight; contribute one's part.*
**English explanation:** To contribute the amount of work or responsibility that is reasonably expected.
1. Each group should do its fair share of the work. — 每个小组都应公平承担自己的工作量。
2. She does more than her fair share. — 她承担的远超自己应尽的份额。

### It's not like
**意思：** “又不是……/并不是说……”，用于反驳假设或缓和解释。类似：*It's not as if ...*
**English explanation:** It isn't the case that; often used to reject an assumption or soften an explanation.
1. It's not like we have to decide today. — 又不是非得今天决定。
2. It's not like the old method never worked. — 并不是说旧方法从来没用过。

### With that being said
**意思：** 尽管如此/话虽如此，引出补充或转折。类似：*that said; nevertheless.*
**English explanation:** Even so; used to introduce a related qualification or contrast.
1. The sample is small. With that being said, the trend is clear. — 样本量很小。尽管如此，趋势还是很明显。
2. It's expensive; with that being said, it saves time. — 它很贵，但话虽如此，它确实省时间。

### Bump into
**意思：** 偶然遇见；也可遇到问题。类似：*run into; come across.*
**English explanation:** To meet someone by chance; it can also mean to encounter a problem.
1. I bumped into my advisor on campus. — 我在校园里偶然碰到了导师。
2. We bumped into a permission error. — 我们遇到了权限错误。

### Settle in
**意思：** 安顿下来；适应新环境。类似：*get comfortable; adjust.*
**English explanation:** To become comfortable in a new place or situation after arriving.
1. It took a few weeks to settle in at the new lab. — 我花了几周才适应新实验室。
2. Come in and settle in wherever you'd like. — 请进，随便找地方坐下安顿一下。

### I'm not sure that's necessarily the case
**意思：** 委婉地表示“未必如此”，对前述判断提出保留。类似：*I'm not convinced that's true.*
**English explanation:** A polite way to say that a previous claim may not be true in every situation.
1. I'm not sure that's necessarily the case for smaller models. — 对较小模型来说，情况未必如此。
2. I'm not sure that's necessarily the case; let's check the data. — 我不确定一定是这样，我们查一下数据吧。

### nice-to-have
**词汇补充：** /ˌnaɪs tə ˈhæv/；常搭配 *a nice-to-have feature/option*；近义 *optional extra*；反义 *must-have*。
**English explanation:** Useful or desirable, but not essential.
**意思：** 有了更好但不是必需的功能/东西。类似：*optional extra; desirable but nonessential.*
1. Dark mode is a nice-to-have, not a release blocker. — 深色模式有更好，但不是发布的必要条件。
2. The extra chart is a nice-to-have. — 这张额外图表有了更好。

### must-have
**词汇补充：** /ˈmʌstˌhæv/；常搭配 *a must-have feature/tool*；近义 *essential, necessity*；反义 *nice-to-have, optional extra*。
**English explanation:** Essential or necessary; something that should not be left out.
**意思：** 必不可少的东西/功能。类似：*essential; required.*
1. Offline access is a must-have for field work. — 离线访问是野外工作的必备功能。
2. Reliability is a must-have for this service. — 可靠性是这项服务的必需条件。

### hash out
**意思：** 通过讨论把细节/分歧解决清楚。类似：*work out; settle through discussion.*
**English explanation:** To discuss details or disagreements until they are resolved.
1. We hashed out the API design this morning. — 我们今天上午讨论敲定了 API 设计。
2. The teams need to hash out the ownership question. — 两个团队需要把责任归属问题讨论清楚。

### take over
**意思：** 接管、接手；也可指取代/占据。类似：*assume control; take charge.*
**English explanation:** To assume control or responsibility; it can also mean replace or dominate.
1. Maya will take over the project next month. — Maya 下个月会接手这个项目。
2. The new process takes over after the migration. — 迁移后新流程会取代旧流程。

### catch up on
**意思：** 补上落下的内容/进度。类似：*get up to date on; make up for missed work.*
**English explanation:** To do or review something that was missed or left unfinished.
1. I need to catch up on the readings. — 我得补上落下的阅读。
2. Let's catch up on what changed while I was away. — 我们聊聊我不在时有哪些变化。

### take the lead on
**意思：** 主导/牵头负责某项工作。类似：*lead; take charge of.*
**English explanation:** To be the person who guides or is primarily responsible for a task.
1. He took the lead on the evaluation. — 他牵头负责评估工作。
2. Would you take the lead on organizing the workshop? — 你愿意牵头组织研讨会吗？

### commit to
**意思：** 承诺/投入去做；也可指提交代码到版本库。类似：*pledge to; dedicate oneself to.*
**English explanation:** To promise or dedicate oneself to doing something; in software, to save changes to version control.
1. We committed to sharing the data. — 我们承诺共享数据。
2. She committed the fix to the repository. — 她把修复提交到了代码库。

### lean toward
**意思：** 倾向于某个选择或观点。类似：*favor; be inclined toward.*
**English explanation:** To prefer or be more likely to choose one option or opinion.
1. I'm leaning toward the simpler design. — 我更倾向于简单的设计。
2. The evidence leans toward a network issue. — 证据更倾向于说明是网络问题。

### right away
**意思：** 立刻、马上。类似：*immediately; at once.*
**English explanation:** Immediately or without delay.
1. I'll send the file right away. — 我马上把文件发过去。
2. The system rejected the request right away. — 系统立即拒绝了请求。

### fraternity
**词汇补充：** /frəˈtɜrnəti/；常搭配 *join a fraternity, college fraternity*；近义 *brotherhood, student organization*（依语境）；反义无常用词。
**English explanation:** A social organization for male students at a college or university; more generally, a group with shared interests.
/frəˈtɜrnəti/ **n.** （大学）兄弟会；也指团体或行业群体。类似：*student fraternity; association.*
1. He joined a fraternity during his first year. — 他大一时加入了一个兄弟会。
2. The academic fraternity discussed the proposal. — 学术界人士讨论了这个提案。

## Specialized

### It's on the house
**意思：** 由店家免费招待。类似：*It's free; it's complimentary.*
**English explanation:** The business is providing it for free.
1. Dessert is on the house tonight. — 今晚甜点由店里招待。
2. The café gave us coffee on the house. — 咖啡馆请我们喝了咖啡。

### I'm in hot water
**意思：** 我惹上麻烦了。类似：*I'm in trouble; I'm in a tight spot.*
**English explanation:** I'm in trouble or likely to face criticism or punishment.
1. I'm in hot water because I missed the deadline. — 我因为错过截止日期惹上麻烦了。
2. He was in hot water with his supervisor. — 他惹得主管很不高兴。

### You have my word
**意思：** 我向你保证。类似：*I promise; you can count on me.*
**English explanation:** I promise; you can trust that I will do what I said.
1. You have my word that I'll keep it confidential. — 我向你保证会保密。
2. You have my word—we'll finish the review today. — 我向你保证，我们今天会完成审查。

### It's a steal
**意思：** 非常划算、便宜得像捡到宝。类似：*It's a bargain; great value.*
**English explanation:** It's an unusually good bargain or very low price.
1. At that price, the laptop is a steal. — 这个价格买这台笔记本太划算了。
2. The tickets were a steal at half price. — 半价买到这些票真是捡到便宜了。

### Beat around the bush
**意思：** 拐弯抹角、不直说。类似：*avoid getting to the point.*
**English explanation:** To avoid saying something directly or delay getting to the main point.
1. Stop beating around the bush and tell me the issue. — 别拐弯抹角了，告诉我问题是什么。
2. He beat around the bush instead of asking directly. — 他没有直接问，而是绕来绕去。

### Push one's luck
**意思：** 得寸进尺、冒险要求过多。类似：*take an unnecessary risk; overdo it.*
**English explanation:** To take an unnecessary risk by asking for or expecting too much.
1. You got an extension; don't push your luck by asking for another. — 你已经延期了，别得寸进尺再要一次。
2. We should leave now rather than push our luck with the weather. — 我们现在就走，别冒险赌天气不会变。

### Jump the queue
**意思：** 插队；抢先获得处理。英式英语更常见。类似：*cut in line (North American).*
**English explanation:** To move ahead of others who have been waiting; more common in British English.
1. He tried to jump the queue at the ticket counter. — 他试图在售票处插队。
2. Premium requests do not jump the queue automatically. — 高优先级请求不会自动插队。

### Cut in line
**意思：** 插队，北美常用。类似：*jump the queue.*
**English explanation:** To move ahead of people who have been waiting; common in North American English.
1. Please don't cut in line. — 请不要插队。
2. Someone cut in line while we were waiting. — 我们排队时有人插了队。

### On the dot
**意思：** 正点、分秒不差。类似：*exactly on time.*
**English explanation:** At exactly the stated time.
1. The meeting starts at nine on the dot. — 会议九点整开始。
2. She arrived at noon on the dot. — 她正午准时到达。

### table something
**意思：** 暂缓讨论/搁置；美式会议语境常用。英式英语中 table 也可表示提交讨论，需注意地区差异。类似：*set aside; postpone.*
**English explanation:** In American meeting use, to postpone discussion of something; in British use, it can mean formally present it for discussion.
1. Let's table the proposal until we have more data. — 等有更多数据后我们再讨论这个提案。
2. The committee tabled the issue for a month. — 委员会把这个问题搁置了一个月。

### park something
**意思：** 暂时搁置一个问题/想法，稍后再处理。类似：*put aside; defer.*
**English explanation:** To set an issue or idea aside temporarily so it can be considered later.
1. Let's park that question and finish the agenda. — 我们先把那个问题搁置，继续完成议程。
2. We parked the redesign until the migration was done. — 迁移完成前，我们暂缓了重设计。

### go down a rabbit hole
**意思：** 钻进某个复杂话题/细节里，越查越深，常偏离原任务。类似：*get sidetracked; dive too deeply into something.*
**English explanation:** To become deeply involved in a complicated topic, often losing sight of the original task.
1. I went down a rabbit hole reading old bug reports. — 我翻旧 bug 报告越看越深，结果跑题了。
2. Don't go down a rabbit hole before the deadline. — 截止日期前别陷进细节里出不来。

### pencil something in
**意思：** 暂定安排某事，之后可能调整。类似：*tentatively schedule.*
**English explanation:** To put something provisionally on a schedule, with the possibility of changing it.
1. Let's pencil in Thursday for the review. — 我们暂定周四审查。
2. I penciled the appointment in for the afternoon. — 我暂定把预约安排在下午。

### block off time
**意思：** 在日程上留出/锁定一段时间。类似：*reserve time; set aside time.*
**English explanation:** To reserve a period in a schedule for a particular purpose.
1. I blocked off an hour for writing. — 我留出一小时写作。
2. Block off Friday morning for the lab meeting. — 把周五上午留给实验室会议。

### get the ball rolling
**意思：** 让事情启动、开始推进。类似：*get started; initiate progress.*
**English explanation:** To start an activity or process so that progress can begin.
1. Let's get the ball rolling with a short pilot. — 我们先做个小试点启动起来。
2. She got the ball rolling by sending the first draft. — 她发出初稿，推动事情开始了。

### get something off the ground
**意思：** 让计划/项目成功启动。类似：*launch; get started successfully.*
**English explanation:** To make a project or plan begin operating successfully.
1. We need funding to get the project off the ground. — 我们需要资金让项目启动。
2. The team got the new service off the ground in six months. — 团队用了六个月让新服务正式运作起来。

### ahead of schedule
**意思：** 进度提前。类似：*early; ahead of plan.*
**English explanation:** Earlier or progressing faster than the planned timeline.
1. The migration finished ahead of schedule. — 迁移提前完成了。
2. We're two days ahead of schedule. — 我们比计划提前了两天。

### behind schedule
**意思：** 进度落后。类似：*late; behind plan.*
**English explanation:** Later or progressing more slowly than the planned timeline.
1. The project is a week behind schedule. — 项目进度落后一周。
2. We fell behind schedule after the server failure. — 服务器故障后我们进度落后了。

### bite off more than you can chew
**意思：** 接下超出能力/时间范围的任务。类似：*take on too much; overcommit.*
**English explanation:** To accept more work or responsibility than you can manage.
1. I bit off more than I could chew with three courses and a job. — 我同时上三门课又工作，接的事情太多了。
2. Don't bite off more than you can chew this semester. — 这学期别揽下超出能力的事情。

### own something
**意思：** 对某事负责并承认其结果；也可指拥有。类似：*take responsibility for; be accountable for.*
**English explanation:** To accept responsibility for something and its results; it can also mean possess it.
1. He owned the mistake and fixed it. — 他承认并承担了错误，然后修复了它。
2. I own this laptop. — 这台笔记本是我的。

### Hang on.
**意思：** 等一下；也可指抓紧。类似：*wait a moment; hold on.*
**English explanation:** Wait briefly; it can also mean hold tightly.
1. Hang on—I need to save this file. — 等一下，我得保存这个文件。
2. Hang on to the rail while you go down. — 下去时抓紧扶手。

### you know what I mean
**意思：** 你懂我意思吧；用于确认理解，也常作口头填充语。类似：*if you see what I mean.*
**English explanation:** A conversational phrase used to check whether someone understands, or as an informal filler.
1. The explanation is simple but not simplistic, you know what I mean? — 这个解释简单但不简陋，你懂我的意思吧？
2. It felt a little off, you know what I mean? — 感觉有点不对劲，你明白吧？
