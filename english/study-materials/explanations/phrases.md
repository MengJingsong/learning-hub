# Phrase Explanations

Each entry gives the Chinese meaning, a similar expression, and two contextual examples with translations. Patterns such as X/Y are placeholders.

## Core

### bottom line
**意思：** 最重要的结论；口语中也可指“总而言之”。类似：*the main point*.
**English explanation:** The most important point or final conclusion; it can also introduce a brief summary.
1. The bottom line is that we need more data. — 最重要的是我们还需要更多数据。
2. Bottom line, the service is not ready. — 总而言之，这项服务还没准备好。

### flesh out
**意思：** 补充细节，使想法、计划或草稿更完整。类似：*develop; elaborate on*.
**English explanation:** To add details so that an idea, plan, or draft becomes clearer and more complete.
1. We need to flesh out the evaluation plan. — 我们需要把评估计划补充完整。
2. She fleshed out the proposal with two examples. — 她用两个例子充实了提案。

### nail down
**意思：** 最终确定或弄清细节。类似：*finalize; pin down*.
**English explanation:** To determine or finalize something precisely, such as a date, requirement, or decision.
1. Let's nail down the meeting time today. — 我们今天把会议时间定下来吧。
2. The team has not nailed down the root cause yet. — 团队还没确定根本原因。

### in scope / out of scope
**意思：** 在范围内 / 不在当前范围内。类似：*included / excluded from the scope*.
**English explanation:** Included within the defined work or topic / excluded from that defined work or topic.
1. Security testing is in scope for this release. — 安全测试属于本次发布范围。
2. A mobile redesign is out of scope. — 移动端重设计不在当前范围内。

### at a glance
**意思：** 一看便知；快速浏览时。类似：*quickly; at first sight*.
**English explanation:** Quickly or immediately, with only a brief look.
1. The chart lets us compare the results at a glance. — 这张图让我们一眼就能比较结果。
2. At a glance, the two versions look identical. — 乍一看，这两个版本完全相同。

### in parallel
**意思：** 同时并行地进行。类似：*concurrently; at the same time*.
**English explanation:** At the same time, especially when separate tasks proceed concurrently.
1. We ran both experiments in parallel. — 我们并行进行了两个实验。
2. The workers process requests in parallel. — 工作进程并行处理请求。

### up to date
**意思：** 最新的；保持更新的。类似：*current; updated*.
**English explanation:** Containing the latest information or reflecting the current state.
1. Make sure the dependency list is up to date. — 确保依赖项列表是最新的。
2. I keep the documentation up to date. — 我会持续更新文档。

### regardless of X
**意思：** 不论 X 如何；不受 X 影响。类似：*irrespective of X*.
**English explanation:** Without being affected or changed by X; no matter what X is.
1. The policy applies regardless of team size. — 无论团队规模如何，这项政策都适用。
2. We record every failure regardless of its cause. — 无论原因是什么，我们都会记录每次故障。

### with respect to X
**意思：** 关于/就 X 而言，正式语体。类似：*regarding X; in terms of X*.
**English explanation:** Concerning X or when considering X; a relatively formal phrase.
1. We found no change with respect to latency. — 就延迟而言，我们没有发现变化。
2. The rules differ with respect to data retention. — 关于数据保留，规则有所不同。

### as opposed to X
**意思：** 而非 X；用于对比。类似：*rather than X; in contrast with X*.
**English explanation:** In contrast with X, or rather than X.
1. We measured completed jobs, as opposed to submitted jobs. — 我们测量的是已完成的任务，而非已提交的任务。
2. The class focuses on practice as opposed to theory. — 这门课侧重实践而非理论。

### rule X out
**意思：** 排除 X 这种可能性。类似：*eliminate; exclude*.
**English explanation:** To decide that X is not possible or should not be considered.
1. These results rule out a simple scaling explanation. — 这些结果排除了简单的扩展性解释。
2. We cannot rule out measurement error. — 我们不能排除测量误差。

### hold X fixed
**意思：** 保持 X 不变。类似：*keep X constant*.
**English explanation:** To keep X unchanged while other factors vary.
1. Hold the input size fixed during the comparison. — 比较时保持输入大小不变。
2. We held temperature fixed across all trials. — 所有试验中我们都保持温度不变。

### under sustained load
**意思：** 在持续负载下。类似：*under continuous/heavy load*.
**English explanation:** While a system continues to handle a workload over an extended period.
1. The queue grows under sustained load. — 持续负载下队列会增长。
2. The service remained stable under sustained load. — 服务在持续负载下保持稳定。

### by default
**意思：** 默认情况下。类似：*as a standard setting; unless changed*.
**English explanation:** In the usual setting or unless someone chooses a different option.
1. Logging is enabled by default. — 日志记录默认开启。
2. By default, the tool uses the latest version. — 该工具默认使用最新版本。

### take effect
**意思：** 开始生效。类似：*come into effect; become active*.
**English explanation:** To begin to operate, apply, or produce a result.
1. The change will take effect after a restart. — 重启后更改将生效。
2. The new rate limit took effect at noon. — 新的速率限制在中午开始生效。

### keep pace with X
**意思：** 跟上 X 的速度或发展。类似：*keep up with X*.
**English explanation:** To move, develop, or progress as quickly as X.
1. The infrastructure must keep pace with demand. — 基础设施必须跟上需求增长。
2. She reads papers to keep pace with the field. — 她通过阅读论文跟进领域发展。

### fall back to X
**意思：** 主方案不可用时退回到 X。类似：*revert to X; use X as a backup*.
**English explanation:** To use X as a backup when the preferred option is unavailable or fails.
1. The client falls back to HTTP/1.1 if needed. — 必要时客户端会退回到 HTTP/1.1。
2. If the cache misses, we fall back to the database. — 如果缓存未命中，我们就回退到数据库。

### run out of X
**意思：** 用完/耗尽 X。类似：*use up; exhaust*.
**English explanation:** To use all of X so that none remains.
1. We ran out of memory during the test. — 测试期间内存耗尽了。
2. The team is running out of time. — 团队快没时间了。

### on demand
**意思：** 按需提供或触发。类似：*as needed; when requested*.
**English explanation:** Available or performed when it is requested or needed.
1. The report is generated on demand. — 报告按需生成。
2. Users can load the model on demand. — 用户可以按需加载模型。

### up front
**意思：** 预先；提前；也可指在前面。类似：*in advance; beforehand*.
**English explanation:** Before something begins or is completed; in advance.
1. We agreed on the cost up front. — 我们事先谈好了费用。
2. State the assumptions up front. — 把假设提前说明。

### be consistent with X
**意思：** 与 X 一致/相符。类似：*agree with; align with*.
**English explanation:** To agree with X or not contradict it.
1. The measurements are consistent with the model. — 测量结果与模型相符。
2. Her account is consistent with the logs. — 她的叙述与日志一致。

### so far
**意思：** 到目前为止。类似：*up to now; thus far*.
**English explanation:** Until now, or up to the present point.
1. So far, the tests have passed. — 到目前为止，测试都通过了。
2. We have found no evidence so far. — 目前我们还没有发现证据。

### in principle
**意思：** 理论上/原则上；实际是否可行尚未必定。类似：*theoretically; conceptually*.
**English explanation:** In theory or according to a general idea, even if practical details remain.
1. In principle, the method works for any graph. — 原则上，该方法适用于任何图。
2. The change is possible in principle but costly. — 原则上可以这样改，但代价很高。

### in practice
**意思：** 实际上；在真实情况下。类似：*in reality; operationally*.
**English explanation:** In actual use or real situations, rather than only in theory.
1. In practice, network delays vary. — 实际上，网络延迟会变化。
2. The rule is simple in theory but difficult in practice. — 这条规则理论上简单，实际操作却很难。

### dig into
**意思：** 深入调查或仔细研究。类似：*investigate; examine closely*.
**English explanation:** To examine or investigate something carefully and in detail.
1. Let's dig into the failure logs. — 我们深入查一下故障日志。
2. The paper digs into the causes of bias. — 论文深入探讨了偏差的成因。

### follow up on
**意思：** 跟进某事/进一步处理。类似：*pursue; check back on*.
**English explanation:** To take further action or check again about something previously discussed.
1. I'll follow up on the request tomorrow. — 我明天会跟进这个请求。
2. We need to follow up on the reviewers' concerns. — 我们需要进一步处理审稿人的顾虑。

### come down to
**意思：** 归根结底取决于/归结为。类似：*boil down to*.
**English explanation:** To have as its main cause, issue, or deciding factor.
1. The choice comes down to cost and reliability. — 这个选择归根结底取决于成本和可靠性。
2. It comes down to whether the data are representative. — 归根结底要看数据是否具有代表性。

### run into
**意思：** 遇到问题/偶然碰到；意义依语境而定。类似：*encounter; come across*.
**English explanation:** To encounter a problem or situation, or to meet someone by chance.
1. We ran into a memory limit. — 我们遇到了内存上限问题。
2. I ran into Maya at the seminar. — 我在研讨会上偶然遇到了 Maya。

### rule of thumb
**意思：** 实用经验法则，并非严格定律。类似：*practical guideline; rough rule*.
**English explanation:** A practical general guideline based on experience, not a strict rule.
1. As a rule of thumb, keep functions small. — 经验上，函数最好保持简短。
2. A useful rule of thumb is to test one change at a time. — 一个实用经验是一次只测试一个改动。

### circle back to
**意思：** 稍后重新讨论/处理某事，常见于工作交流。类似：*return to; revisit*.
**English explanation:** To return to a topic or task later, often after discussing something else.
1. Let's circle back to the budget next week. — 我们下周再讨论预算。
2. I'll circle back to your question after the demo. — 演示结束后我会再回答你的问题。

### wrap up
**意思：** 结束或收尾；也可指总结。类似：*finish; conclude*.
**English explanation:** To finish an activity or bring it to a conclusion.
1. Let's wrap up the meeting with next steps. — 我们以讨论后续安排来结束会议吧。
2. She wrapped up the analysis yesterday. — 她昨天完成了分析。

### bandwidth
**词汇补充：** /ˈbændˌwɪdθ/；常搭配 *network bandwidth, bandwidth usage, have the bandwidth to do*；近义 *capacity, available time*（比喻义）；反义视语境为 *bandwidth shortage*。
**English explanation:** The capacity to transmit data; informally, the time or mental capacity available for work.
**意思：** 字面指带宽；引申为可用时间、精力或处理能力。类似：*capacity; available time*.
1. The link has enough bandwidth for the video. — 这条链路有足够带宽传输视频。
2. I don't have the bandwidth to take on another project. — 我没有精力再接一个项目。

### lay out
**意思：** 清楚地陈述、安排或展示。类似：*set out; present*.
**English explanation:** To present information clearly, arrange a plan, or display items.
1. The paper lays out three hypotheses. — 论文清楚列出了三个假设。
2. Let's lay out the options before deciding. — 决定前先把选项列出来。

### walk through
**意思：** 逐步讲解或演示。类似：*go through; explain step by step*.
**English explanation:** To explain or demonstrate something step by step.
1. Can you walk me through the setup? — 你能带我一步步了解设置吗？
2. The tutorial walks through a complete example. — 这个教程逐步演示了一个完整例子。

### take X into account
**意思：** 把 X 纳入考虑。类似：*consider; factor in X*.
**English explanation:** To consider X when making a decision or judgment.
1. The estimate takes maintenance costs into account. — 这个估算考虑了维护成本。
2. We should take the sample size into account. — 我们应该把样本量考虑进去。

### in terms of X
**意思：** 就 X 而言；从 X 这个方面看。类似：*with regard to X; by X measure*.
**English explanation:** When viewed or discussed in relation to X.
1. The new design is better in terms of latency. — 从延迟来看，新设计更好。
2. In terms of cost, both options are similar. — 就成本而言，两个方案差不多。

### compared with X
**意思：** 与 X 相比。类似：*compared to X; in comparison with X*.
**English explanation:** When one thing is considered alongside X to identify similarities or differences.
1. Compared with last year, the error rate is lower. — 与去年相比，错误率更低。
2. The model is small compared with its predecessor. — 与前代相比，这个模型更小。

### under realistic conditions
**意思：** 在符合现实情况的条件下。类似：*in real-world settings*.
**English explanation:** In circumstances that closely resemble actual use or real life.
1. We tested the system under realistic conditions. — 我们在接近真实的条件下测试了系统。
2. The method may behave differently under realistic conditions. — 在真实条件下，该方法的表现可能不同。

### at scale
**意思：** 在大规模情况下/大规模地。类似：*on a large scale; at large scale*.
**English explanation:** At a large size or across many users, machines, or cases.
1. The approach is difficult to operate at scale. — 这种方法难以大规模运维。
2. We need to validate the design at scale. — 我们需要大规模验证这个设计。

### in production
**意思：** 在生产环境中运行/使用。类似：*in a live environment*.
**English explanation:** Running as part of a live service or real operating environment.
1. The feature has been in production for months. — 这项功能已在生产环境运行数月。
2. Never test destructive commands in production. — 不要在生产环境测试破坏性命令。

### lead to X
**意思：** 导致 X；X 是结果。类似：*result in X; cause X*.
**English explanation:** To cause X or make X happen.
1. A stale cache can lead to incorrect results. — 过期缓存可能导致错误结果。
2. Poor documentation led to confusion. — 文档不充分造成了困惑。

### result in X
**意思：** 造成 X，强调结果。类似：*lead to X; bring about X*.
**English explanation:** To cause X as an outcome.
1. The change resulted in lower overhead. — 这项改动带来了更低的开销。
2. A configuration error may result in an outage. — 配置错误可能导致服务中断。

### result from X
**意思：** 由 X 引起；X 是原因。类似：*arise from X; be caused by X*.
**English explanation:** To happen because of X; X is the cause.
1. The delay resulted from network congestion. — 延迟是由网络拥塞引起的。
2. Most failures result from invalid input. — 大多数故障源于无效输入。

### remain an open question
**意思：** 仍是尚未解决的问题。类似：*remain unresolved; still be unclear*.
**English explanation:** To continue to be unresolved or not yet answered.
1. Whether the effect generalizes remains an open question. — 这种效应能否推广仍未确定。
2. The best threshold remains an open question. — 最合适的阈值仍未确定。

### in contrast to X
**意思：** 与 X 相比/相反。类似：*unlike X; as opposed to X*.
**English explanation:** Unlike X, especially in a way that highlights a difference.
1. In contrast to the baseline, this version uses less memory. — 与基线相比，这个版本占用的内存更少。
2. In contrast to earlier work, we include noisy samples. — 与早期工作不同，我们纳入了带噪样本。

### take a closer look at X
**意思：** 更仔细地检查 X。类似：*examine X more closely*.
**English explanation:** To examine X more carefully or in greater detail.
1. Let's take a closer look at the failure case. — 我们仔细看看这个失败案例。
2. The authors take a closer look at subgroup effects. — 作者进一步仔细分析了各子组效应。

### or so
**意思：** 左右、大约；放在数量或时间之后，语气非正式。类似：*approximately; about*.
**English explanation:** Approximately; used after a number or amount to indicate an estimate.
1. It takes ten minutes or so. — 大约需要十分钟。
2. We invited thirty people or so. — 我们邀请了大约三十个人。

### reach capacity
**意思：** 达到容量上限。类似：*fill up; hit the limit*.
**English explanation:** To become full or reach the maximum amount that can be handled.
1. The queue reached capacity during the peak. — 高峰期间队列达到了容量上限。
2. Once the server reaches capacity, new requests are rejected. — 服务器达到容量上限后会拒绝新请求。

### leave X out
**意思：** 省略或不包括 X。类似：*omit; exclude X*.
**English explanation:** To omit or exclude X from something.
1. Leave the optional field out if it is unknown. — 如果不知道这个可选字段，就省略它。
2. The analysis left out two incomplete records. — 分析排除了两条不完整记录。

## Useful

### iron out
**意思：** 解决小问题、消除分歧或把细节理顺。类似：*resolve; smooth out*.
**English explanation:** To resolve small problems or differences and make a plan or process work smoothly.
1. We need to iron out a few issues before launch. — 发布前我们要解决几个问题。
2. They ironed out the differences in the draft. — 他们理顺了草稿中的分歧。

### proxy for
**意思：** 作为 X 的间接指标或替代测量。类似：*stand-in measure for; indicator of*.
**English explanation:** Something used as an indirect measure or substitute for another thing.
1. Clicks are a poor proxy for user satisfaction. — 点击量并不是用户满意度的好替代指标。
2. We use temperature as a proxy for workload intensity. — 我们用温度作为工作负载强度的间接指标。

### safety net
**意思：** 安全保障/兜底措施。类似：*fallback; protective measure*.
**English explanation:** A backup or protective measure that reduces the harm if something goes wrong.
1. The backup is a safety net if deployment fails. — 如果部署失败，备份就是兜底保障。
2. Add a safety net before changing the schema. — 修改架构前加一道安全保障。

### in case
**意思：** 以防；也可引出某种情况。类似：*as a precaution; if it happens that*.
**English explanation:** As a precaution against a possible event; it can also introduce a situation that may occur.
1. Save a copy in case the update fails. — 另存一份，以防更新失败。
2. In case you missed it, the deadline has moved. — 如果你还没看到，截止日期已经改了。

### keep around
**意思：** 留着备用/保留在身边。类似：*retain; keep available*.
**English explanation:** To retain something nearby or available for possible future use.
1. Keep the old configuration around for rollback. — 把旧配置留着以便回滚。
2. I keep a notebook around for quick ideas. — 我会留本笔记本随手记想法。

### cut across
**意思：** 横跨多个领域/群体，或穿过某个区域。类似：*span; cross*.
**English explanation:** To extend across several groups or areas, or to cross something physically.
1. The issue cuts across several research areas. — 这个问题横跨多个研究领域。
2. The path cuts across the campus. — 这条路穿过校园。

### trace X back to Y
**意思：** 追溯 X 的来源至 Y。类似：*track X to Y; attribute X to Y*.
**English explanation:** To find the origin or cause of X and identify Y as its source.
1. We traced the error back to a parsing change. — 我们把错误追溯到一次解析逻辑改动。
2. The team traced the delay back to a saturated queue. — 团队将延迟追溯到已饱和的队列。

### be subject to X
**意思：** 受 X 影响/制约；也可指需遵守 X。类似：*be affected by; be governed by*.
**English explanation:** To be affected, controlled, or governed by X.
1. Results are subject to sampling error. — 结果会受到抽样误差影响。
2. All refunds are subject to approval. — 所有退款都需经过批准。

### in passing
**意思：** 顺带地、简略地提到。类似：*briefly; as an aside*.
**English explanation:** Briefly and as a secondary point, rather than as the main topic.
1. She mentioned the issue in passing. — 她顺带提到了这个问题。
2. The limitation is noted in passing, not analyzed. — 这个局限只是顺带提及，并未分析。

### from scratch
**意思：** 从头开始；从零开始。类似：*from the beginning; from the ground up*.
**English explanation:** Starting from the beginning, without using an existing version or preparation.
1. We built the prototype from scratch. — 我们从头搭建了原型。
2. She learned Python from scratch. — 她从零开始学习 Python。

### one at a time
**意思：** 一次一个。类似：*individually; in sequence*.
**English explanation:** Individually, with only one item being handled at once.
1. Change one parameter at a time. — 一次只改一个参数。
2. The system handles files one at a time. — 系统一次处理一个文件。

### in place
**意思：** 已就位/已实施；也可指在原处。类似：*established; in position*.
**English explanation:** Already established or operating; it can also mean physically in its position.
1. The safeguards are already in place. — 安全措施已经到位。
2. Leave the connector in place during the test. — 测试期间让连接器保持原位。

### at most
**意思：** 至多、不超过。类似：*no more than*.
**English explanation:** Not more than a specified amount or number.
1. The job takes at most five minutes. — 这项任务最多需要五分钟。
2. At most two workers can access the device. — 最多两名工作进程可以访问该设备。

### tell X apart
**意思：** 区分 X 与另一对象。类似：*distinguish; differentiate*.
**English explanation:** To recognize the difference between X and another similar thing.
1. The two versions are hard to tell apart. — 这两个版本很难区分。
2. Color helps users tell the states apart. — 颜色帮助用户区分不同状态。

### at the cost of X
**意思：** 以牺牲 X 为代价。类似：*at the expense of X*.
**English explanation:** By giving up or harming X in order to gain something else.
1. The shortcut improves speed at the cost of accuracy. — 这个捷径以牺牲准确度为代价提高了速度。
2. We reduced memory use at the cost of extra computation. — 我们以增加计算量为代价降低了内存使用。

### trade X for Y
**意思：** 用 X 换取 Y，常暗示取舍。类似：*exchange X for Y; sacrifice X to gain Y*.
**English explanation:** To exchange X for Y, often accepting a disadvantage in return for a benefit.
1. We traded a little speed for better reliability. — 我们牺牲了一点速度来换取更好的可靠性。
2. Don't trade clarity for brevity. — 不要为了简短而牺牲清晰度。

### on its own
**意思：** 独自地；本身；无需外力。类似：*by itself; independently*.
**English explanation:** By itself, independently, or without help from anything else.
1. The process can recover on its own. — 这个进程可以自行恢复。
2. The score on its own does not prove the claim. — 单凭这个分数本身不能证明该主张。

### at the cost of
**意思：** 以……为代价；后接名词或动名词。类似：*at the expense of*.
**English explanation:** By sacrificing something valuable in order to obtain another benefit.
1. The design saves power at the cost of throughput. — 该设计以牺牲吞吐量为代价节省电力。
2. They expanded quickly at the cost of stability. — 他们以稳定性为代价迅速扩张。

### end up
**意思：** 最终处于某状态或做了某事，常指非计划结果。类似：*eventually become/do*.
**English explanation:** To finally reach a state or do something, often unexpectedly or without planning it.
1. We ended up using the simpler method. — 我们最后还是用了更简单的方法。
2. If we wait, we may end up missing the deadline. — 如果一直等，我们可能最后会错过截止日期。

### bring up
**意思：** 提出话题；也可指抚养或调出屏幕。类似：*raise; mention*.
**English explanation:** To mention a subject; depending on context, it can also mean raise a child or display something.
1. She brought up a concern about privacy. — 她提出了一个关于隐私的顾虑。
2. Can you bring up the dashboard? — 你能打开仪表板吗？

### hold up
**意思：** 支撑/经受住；也可指延误。类似：*withstand; delay*.
**English explanation:** To remain strong under pressure or scrutiny; it can also mean to delay something.
1. The hypothesis does not hold up under scrutiny. — 这个假设经不起仔细审查。
2. Traffic held up the delivery. — 交通延误了配送。

### poke around
**意思：** 随意查看或探索，语气非正式。类似：*look around; explore*.
**English explanation:** To explore or look through a place or system informally and without a fixed plan.
1. I poked around the repository to find the config. — 我在代码库里翻找配置文件。
2. Feel free to poke around the demo. — 可以随意试用这个演示。

### swap out
**意思：** 替换掉某个部件或选项。类似：*replace; switch out*.
**English explanation:** To remove one item and put another in its place.
1. We swapped out the failing disk. — 我们换掉了故障磁盘。
2. You can swap out the default theme. — 你可以替换默认主题。

### roll out
**意思：** 推出/部署新产品或变更。类似：*launch; deploy*.
**English explanation:** To introduce or deploy a product, service, or change, often gradually.
1. The team rolled out the update gradually. — 团队逐步推出了更新。
2. We will roll out the feature to all users next week. — 我们下周会向所有用户推出该功能。

### phase out
**意思：** 分阶段逐步淘汰。类似：*retire gradually; discontinue gradually*.
**English explanation:** To stop using or providing something gradually over time.
1. The company is phasing out the old API. — 公司正在逐步淘汰旧 API。
2. We phased out the manual process over six months. — 我们在六个月内逐步取消了手工流程。

### carry over
**意思：** 延续/沿用到下一阶段；也可指结转。类似：*transfer; continue into*.
**English explanation:** To continue or transfer something into a later period or different context.
1. The delay carried over into the next release. — 延迟影响到了下一次发布。
2. Unused funds will carry over to next year. — 未用资金将结转到明年。

### make some headway
**意思：** 取得一些进展。类似：*make progress; move forward*.
**English explanation:** To make noticeable progress toward a goal.
1. We made some headway on the evaluation. — 我们在评估方面取得了一些进展。
2. The team made little headway until the bug was isolated. — 在定位到 bug 之前，团队进展不大。

### prioritize
**词汇补充：** /praɪˈɔrəˌtaɪz/；常搭配 *prioritize tasks, prioritize A over B, high-priority*；近义 *rank, give precedence to*；反义 *deprioritize, neglect*。
**English explanation:** To decide which tasks or goals are most important and handle them first.
**意思：** 优先处理/排列优先级。类似：*rank by importance; give priority to*.
1. We should prioritize the overdue items. — 我们应该优先处理逾期事项。
2. The team prioritized reliability over new features. — 团队把可靠性置于新功能之前。

### come at the cost of
**意思：** 以……为代价发生或实现。类似：*come at the expense of*.
**English explanation:** To require the loss or sacrifice of something else as a consequence.
1. The speedup came at the cost of higher memory use. — 这次加速是以更高内存占用为代价实现的。
2. Growth should not come at the cost of user trust. — 增长不应以牺牲用户信任为代价。

### start from scratch
**意思：** 从零开始做。类似：*begin from the beginning; build anew*.
**English explanation:** To create or begin something from the very beginning.
1. We had to start from scratch after the files were corrupted. — 文件损坏后，我们不得不从头开始。
2. She started the analysis from scratch. — 她从零开始做这项分析。

### relative to X
**意思：** 相对于 X；以 X 为参照。类似：*compared with X; with respect to X*.
**English explanation:** In comparison with X or using X as the point of reference.
1. Costs fell relative to the previous design. — 与之前的设计相比，成本下降了。
2. Report each score relative to the baseline. — 把每项分数相对于基线报告。

### under these conditions
**意思：** 在这些条件下。类似：*in this setting; given these conditions*.
**English explanation:** Given the particular circumstances or requirements being discussed.
1. The system remains stable under these conditions. — 在这些条件下，系统仍保持稳定。
2. Under these conditions, the estimate is unbiased. — 在这些条件下，该估算是无偏的。

### beyond the scope of X
**意思：** 超出 X 的范围。类似：*outside the scope of X*.
**English explanation:** Outside the range or limits of X.
1. Security analysis is beyond the scope of this report. — 安全分析超出本报告范围。
2. That question is beyond the scope of our study. — 那个问题超出了我们研究的范围。

### go down the drain
**意思：** 白白浪费/付诸东流，非正式。类似：*be wasted; come to nothing*.
**English explanation:** To be wasted or lost without producing a useful result.
1. Without a backup, months of work could go down the drain. — 没有备份，数月的工作可能会付诸东流。
2. The investment went down the drain after the project was canceled. — 项目取消后，这笔投资打了水漂。

### the elephant in the room
**意思：** 大家都知道却回避不谈的重大问题。类似：*the obvious unaddressed issue*.
**English explanation:** An obvious, important problem that people avoid discussing.
1. The missing data are the elephant in the room. — 缺失数据是那个大家都回避不谈的关键问题。
2. We should address the elephant in the room: the deadline is unrealistic. — 我们应该正视那个关键问题：截止日期不现实。

### limited availability
**意思：** 供应/可用性有限。类似：*scarce availability; limited supply*.
**English explanation:** A situation in which only a small amount or number of something is accessible.
1. The feature is offered with limited availability. — 该功能目前仅有限度开放。
2. Limited availability delayed the deployment. — 可用资源有限，导致部署延迟。

### terms apply
**意思：** 适用相关条款；常见于优惠或服务说明。类似：*subject to conditions*.
**English explanation:** The offer or service is governed by stated conditions or rules.
1. The discount is available through Friday; terms apply. — 折扣周五截止，具体条款适用。
2. Free returns are offered, but terms apply. — 提供免费退货，但需遵守相关条款。

### rejection upheld / rejection overturned
**意思：** 驳回维持原判 / 驳回被推翻；常见于申诉、审查程序。类似：*decision affirmed / reversed*.
**English explanation:** A rejection was confirmed as valid / a rejection was reversed and no longer stands.
1. On appeal, the rejection was upheld. — 上诉后，驳回决定得到维持。
2. The committee overturned the rejection after reviewing new evidence. — 委员会审查新证据后推翻了驳回决定。

### gloss over X
**意思：** 对 X 一带而过或轻描淡写。类似：*skim over; downplay*.
**English explanation:** To mention X quickly while avoiding its important details or problems.
1. The report glosses over the limitations. — 报告对局限性一带而过。
2. Don't gloss over the failed trials. — 不要轻描淡写地略过失败的试验。

### return on investment (ROI)
**意思：** 投资回报；投入相对于收益的回报。类似：*financial return; value gained per investment*.
**English explanation:** The benefit or profit gained compared with the amount invested.
1. We calculated the return on investment for the new hardware. — 我们计算了新硬件的投资回报。
2. The training had a strong return on investment. — 这次培训的投入产出比很高。

### carve X out of Y
**意思：** 从 Y 中划出、分离出 X。类似：*set aside; separate*.
**English explanation:** To create or separate X from a larger group, area, or amount Y.
1. We carved a test partition out of the dataset. — 我们从数据集中划出一个测试分区。
2. The team carved out time for code review. — 团队专门留出了代码审查时间。

## Specialized

### grounds for X
**意思：** X 的理由或依据，常见于正式论证/法律语境。类似：*basis for X; reason to X*.
**English explanation:** Reasons or evidence that justify X, especially a decision or action.
1. The discrepancy is grounds for further investigation. — 这种差异构成进一步调查的理由。
2. There are no grounds for rejecting the result. — 没有理由拒绝这个结果。

### count toward X
**意思：** 计入 X；对 X 的总数或要求有贡献。类似：*contribute to; be included in*.
**English explanation:** To be included as part of the total, progress, or requirement for X.
1. These credits count toward the degree. — 这些学分计入学位要求。
2. Retries do not count toward the request limit. — 重试不计入请求限额。

### be bounded by X
**意思：** 受 X 限定/界定。类似：*be limited by; have X as a bound*.
**English explanation:** To be limited or kept within the limits set by X.
1. Throughput is bounded by disk bandwidth. — 吞吐量受磁盘带宽限制。
2. The error is bounded by the measurement precision. — 误差受测量精度限制。

### in flight
**意思：** 正在进行/尚未完成；技术语境常指请求或交易处理中。类似：*in progress; pending*.
**English explanation:** Currently underway or not yet completed; in computing, a request or operation still being processed.
1. There are three requests in flight. — 目前有三个请求正在处理中。
2. The change is still in flight. — 这项改动仍在进行中。

### fall through to X
**意思：** 前一匹配或方案未生效时，继续落到 X。类似：*default to X; continue to X*.
**English explanation:** To continue to X when an earlier option, match, or condition does not apply.
1. Unknown routes fall through to the default handler. — 未知路由会转到默认处理程序。
2. If the cache misses, the request falls through to storage. — 如果缓存未命中，请求就会继续落到存储层。

### be attributable to X
**意思：** 可归因于 X；正式、谨慎的因果表达。类似：*be due to; result from*.
**English explanation:** To be explainable as caused by X, often stated cautiously.
1. The improvement is attributable to better caching. — 这项改进可归因于更好的缓存策略。
2. The difference cannot be attributed to age alone. — 这种差异不能仅归因于年龄。

### write X up
**意思：** 把 X 写成正式报告/记录。类似：*document; write a report about*.
**English explanation:** To prepare a written report or formal account of X.
1. Please write up the experiment by Friday. — 请在周五前把实验写成报告。
2. He wrote up the incident for the team. — 他为团队整理了事件报告。

### pick up X
**意思：** 取/拿起 X；也可指学习、接收或改善，需看语境。类似：*collect; learn; detect*.
**English explanation:** To lift or collect X; depending on context, to learn, detect, or improve X.
1. I'll pick up the equipment after lunch. — 我午饭后去取设备。
2. The sensor picked up a weak signal. — 传感器检测到了微弱信号。

### carry X over
**意思：** 把 X 延续/转移到下一阶段。类似：*transfer X; continue X into*.
**English explanation:** To transfer X to a later stage or continue using it in another period.
1. Carry the remaining tasks over to next week. — 把剩余任务延到下周。
2. We carried the same assumptions over to the second analysis. — 我们在第二次分析中沿用了相同假设。

### a judgment call
**意思：** 需要个人判断、没有唯一明确规则的决定。类似：*subjective decision; matter of judgment*.
**English explanation:** A decision that depends on personal assessment because no fixed rule gives a single answer.
1. Choosing the cutoff is a judgment call. — 选择截断值需要自行判断。
2. Whether to include the outlier is a judgment call. — 是否纳入这个离群值取决于判断。

### rather than doing X
**意思：** 而不是做 X；rather than 后接动名词与句子中其他结构保持平行。类似：*instead of doing X*.
**English explanation:** Instead of doing X; the following action is presented as the alternative.
1. Rather than adding retries, we increased the timeout. — 我们没有增加重试，而是延长了超时时间。
2. She documented the issue rather than reopening the ticket. — 她记录了问题，而没有重新打开工单。

### as a direct consequence
**意思：** 作为直接后果。类似：*directly as a result*.
**English explanation:** As an immediate result of something, with a clear cause-and-effect link.
1. The key expired and, as a direct consequence, requests failed. — 密钥过期，直接导致请求失败。
2. As a direct consequence of the change, memory use increased. — 这项改动直接导致内存使用增加。

### stop short of X
**意思：** 没有做到/没有达到 X；常表示刻意不进一步采取某行动。类似：*refrain from; not go as far as*.
**English explanation:** To avoid going as far as X or to fail to reach X.
1. The evidence supports an association but stops short of proving causation. — 证据支持相关性，但尚不足以证明因果关系。
2. The team stopped short of removing the feature. — 团队没有进一步采取删除功能的做法。

### within X% of Y
**意思：** 与 Y 相差不超过 X%。类似：*within an X-percent margin of Y*.
**English explanation:** No more than X percent higher or lower than Y.
1. The estimate was within 5% of the measured value. — 估算值与测量值相差不到 5%。
2. Our latency is within 10% of the baseline. — 我们的延迟与基线相差不到 10%。

### wash out
**意思：** 被抵消/消失；也可指彻底冲洗，需看语境。类似：*cancel out; disappear*.
**English explanation:** To cancel or neutralize an effect; in other contexts, to wash something thoroughly.
1. The benefit may wash out under heavy load. — 在高负载下，这项收益可能会消失。
2. Averaging across groups can wash out individual differences. — 跨组求平均可能会掩盖个体差异。

### average out
**意思：** 平均后相互抵消或趋于稳定。类似：*even out; balance out*.
**English explanation:** To become more even or reach a typical value when highs and lows are considered together.
1. The random errors average out over many trials. — 多次试验后随机误差会相互抵消。
2. Costs average out over the full year. — 按全年计算，成本趋于平均。

### level out
**意思：** 趋于平稳/不再上升或下降。类似：*stabilize; plateau*.
**English explanation:** To become stable after rising or falling.
1. Memory use levels out after initialization. — 初始化后内存使用趋于平稳。
2. Enrollment began to level out in the spring. — 春季报名人数开始趋于稳定。

### flatten out
**意思：** 变平/趋于平稳，常用于曲线、增长或地形。类似：*plateau; level off*.
**English explanation:** To become flatter or stop changing as sharply, often describing a trend or curve.
1. The growth curve flattens out after the first month. — 增长曲线在第一个月后趋于平缓。
2. Latency flattened out once the cache warmed up. — 缓存预热后延迟趋于稳定。

### taper off
**意思：** 逐渐减弱或减少。类似：*dwindle; decrease gradually*.
**English explanation:** To decrease gradually in amount, strength, or frequency.
1. Traffic tapers off late at night. — 深夜时流量逐渐减少。
2. The effect tapered off over time. — 这种效应随时间逐渐减弱。

### with the exception of X
**意思：** 除了 X 之外。类似：*except for X; apart from X*.
**English explanation:** Not including X; all the other cases or items are included.
1. All tests passed, with the exception of the integration test. — 除集成测试外，所有测试都通过了。
2. With the exception of one region, results were consistent. — 除一个地区外，结果都一致。

### in the presence of X
**意思：** 在 X 存在/出现时。类似：*when X is present; under X*.
**English explanation:** While X exists or is present.
1. The reaction slows in the presence of oxygen. — 有氧气存在时，反应会变慢。
2. The algorithm remains stable in the presence of noise. — 存在噪声时，该算法仍保持稳定。

### in the wild
**意思：** 在真实环境中，而非受控实验室环境；技术上也可指真实部署。类似：*in real-world use; in production settings*.
**English explanation:** In real-world conditions rather than in a controlled test; for software, in actual use.
1. We have not observed this failure in the wild. — 我们在真实环境中还没观察到这种故障。
2. The feature has been tested in the wild for months. — 这项功能已在真实环境中测试数月。

### at peak
**意思：** 在峰值时/处于最高水平时。类似：*at its maximum; during peak demand*.
**English explanation:** At the highest level or during the time of greatest activity or demand.
1. The service handles 8,000 requests per second at peak. — 峰值时服务每秒处理 8,000 个请求。
2. Power use is highest at peak load. — 峰值负载时功耗最高。

### in line with X
**意思：** 与 X 一致/符合 X。类似：*consistent with; aligned with*.
**English explanation:** Consistent with X or in agreement with its standards or direction.
1. The estimate is in line with prior measurements. — 这个估算与以往测量结果一致。
2. The policy is in line with current guidelines. — 该政策符合现行指南。
