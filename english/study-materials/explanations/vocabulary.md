# Vocabulary Explanations

Pronunciations use common American English IPA. Meanings and synonyms are sense-specific; antonyms are given only when useful. Examples model common academic, technical, and everyday usage.

## Core

### Informative
/ɪnˈfɔrmətɪv/ **adj.** 有信息量的、有教育意义的。搭配：informative article/report/session. 近：educational, insightful; 反：uninformative.
**English explanation:** Giving useful facts or information that help someone understand a subject.
1. The report is informative and easy to follow. — 这份报告信息丰富，也很容易理解。
2. We attended an informative session on research ethics. — 我们参加了一场关于研究伦理的讲解会。

### Rigor
/ˈrɪɡər/ **n.** 严谨；严格。搭配：methodological/academic rigor. 近：thoroughness, precision; 反：sloppiness.
**English explanation:** Careful, exact work that follows high standards and avoids unsupported conclusions.
1. The study is valued for its methodological rigor. — 这项研究因方法严谨而受到认可。
2. We need the same rigor in the evaluation. — 评估也需要同样严谨。

### Fidelity
/fɪˈdɛləti/ **n.** 忠实度、保真度；也可指忠诚。搭配：data/model/representation fidelity. 近：accuracy, faithfulness; 反：distortion.
**English explanation:** The degree to which something accurately preserves or represents its source or intended form.
1. The compressed file preserves image fidelity. — 压缩文件保留了图像保真度。
2. We checked the model's fidelity to the source data. — 我们检查了模型对源数据的忠实度。

### Hedging
/ˈhɛdʒɪŋ/ **n.** 谨慎措辞；避免把结论说得过满。搭配：appropriate/linguistic hedging. 近：qualification, cautious wording; 反：categorical assertion.
**English explanation:** Language that makes a claim less absolute and shows appropriate caution about certainty.
1. “May indicate” is useful hedging when evidence is limited. — 证据有限时，“可能表明”是恰当的谨慎措辞。
2. Excessive hedging can make the conclusion unclear. — 过度使用模糊措辞会让结论不清楚。

### Footprint
/ˈfʊtˌprɪnt/ **n.** 足迹；资源占用规模；影响范围。搭配：memory/carbon/physical footprint. 近：usage, impact; 反：无常用反义词.
**English explanation:** The amount of space, memory, energy, or other resources something uses; it can also mean its effect or reach.
1. The service has a small memory footprint. — 这个服务的内存占用很小。
2. The company is reducing its carbon footprint. — 公司正在减少碳足迹。

### Hinge
/hɪndʒ/ **v./n.** 取决于；铰链。搭配：hinge on/upon a decision. 近：depend on, turn on; 反：be independent of.
**English explanation:** To depend on a particular fact or condition; as a noun, a joint that lets a door or lid swing.
1. The result hinges on the choice of baseline. — 结果取决于基线的选择。
2. Our decision hinges on whether the patch is safe. — 我们的决定取决于这个补丁是否安全。

### Upshot
/ˈʌpˌʃɑt/ **n.** 结果；要点，常指最终结论。搭配：the upshot is that. 近：outcome, bottom line; 反：无常用反义词.
**English explanation:** The final result or main point of a situation or discussion.
1. The upshot is that we need another experiment. — 结果就是我们还需要再做一次实验。
2. The upshot of the meeting was a shorter timeline. — 会议的最终结果是缩短时间表。

### Boilerplate
/ˈbɔɪlərˌpleɪt/ **n./adj.** 套话、固定模板；重复使用的标准文本或代码。搭配：boilerplate code/language. 近：template, standard text; 反：custom-written.
**English explanation:** Standard language, text, or code reused with little change in many places.
1. The contract contains standard boilerplate. — 合同里包含标准套话。
2. The framework removes much of the setup boilerplate. — 这个框架省去了许多样板配置代码。

### Arguably
/ˈɑrɡjuəbli/ **adv.** 可以说；有理由认为（表示判断可辩）。搭配：arguably the best/most important. 近：possibly, it could be argued; 反：undeniably（语气更强）.
**English explanation:** In a way that can reasonably be supported or debated; used to present a defensible opinion.
1. This is arguably the clearest explanation. — 可以说，这是最清楚的解释。
2. The change arguably improves reliability. — 有理由认为这项改动提高了可靠性。

### Triage
/triˈɑʒ/ **n./v.** 分诊；按紧急程度分类处理。搭配：triage issues/requests. 近：prioritize, sort; 反：无常用反义词.
**English explanation:** To sort cases, requests, or problems by urgency so that the most important ones are handled first.
1. We triaged the bugs before the release. — 发布前我们按优先级分诊了这些 bug。
2. The team will triage incoming requests each morning. — 团队每天早上会对新请求分类排序。

### Verdict
/ˈvɜrdɪkt/ **n.** 裁决、判断、结论。搭配：final/verdict on; jury verdict. 近：judgment, conclusion; 反：无常用反义词.
**English explanation:** A formal decision or a considered judgment about something.
1. The verdict was that the feature was not ready. — 最终判断是这项功能还没准备好。
2. The jury reached a verdict after two days. — 陪审团经过两天后作出了裁决。

### Provenance
/ˈprɑvənəns/ **n.** 来源、出处及流转记录，常用于数据或艺术品。搭配：data/source provenance. 近：origin, lineage; 反：unknown origin.
**English explanation:** The origin and history of an object, record, or piece of data, including where it came from.
1. We recorded the provenance of every data point. — 我们记录了每个数据点的来源。
2. The museum verified the painting's provenance. — 博物馆核实了这幅画的来源记录。

### Caveat
/ˈkæviˌæt/ **n.** 注意事项、限制条件。搭配：important caveat; with one caveat. 近：qualification, reservation; 反：unqualified claim.
**English explanation:** A warning, qualification, or condition that limits how a statement or result should be understood.
1. The result is promising, with one caveat. — 结果很有希望，但有一个限制条件。
2. A caveat is that the sample is small. — 需要注意的是样本量很小。

### Reconcile
/ˈrɛkənˌsaɪl/ **v.** 调和、使一致；核对账目/数据。搭配：reconcile differences/accounts/data. 近：resolve, align; 反：conflict, contradict.
**English explanation:** To make two accounts, results, or ideas agree, or to explain their differences; also to balance accounts.
1. We reconciled the two sets of measurements. — 我们核对并调和了两组测量数据。
2. It is hard to reconcile these findings with the earlier report. — 这些发现很难与早期报告相协调。

### Conflate
/kənˈfleɪt/ **v.** 混为一谈，把不同概念合并。搭配：conflate A with B. 近：confuse, merge; 反：distinguish, separate.
**English explanation:** To treat two distinct things as if they were the same.
1. Don't conflate correlation with causation. — 不要把相关性和因果关系混为一谈。
2. The article conflates two different use cases. — 这篇文章把两种不同用例混为一谈。

### Corroboration
/kəˌrɑbəˈreɪʃən/ **n.** 佐证、印证。搭配：independent/strong corroboration. 近：confirmation, support; 反：contradiction.
**English explanation:** Additional evidence that supports or confirms an account, result, or claim.
1. The logs provide independent corroboration. — 日志提供了独立佐证。
2. We need corroboration from another experiment. — 我们需要另一项实验来印证。

### Counterfactual
/ˌkaʊntərˈfæktʃuəl/ **n./adj.** 反事实（与实际事实相反的假设情形）。搭配：counterfactual analysis/scenario. 近：hypothetical alternative; 反：factual account.
**English explanation:** A real or imagined situation describing what would have happened if the actual facts had been different.
1. The analysis compares the outcome with a counterfactual. — 该分析将结果与反事实情形作比较。
2. We asked what would have happened under the counterfactual policy. — 我们探讨了在反事实政策下会发生什么。

### constraint
/kənˈstreɪnt/ **n.** 限制、约束条件。搭配：resource/design/technical constraint. 近：limitation, restriction; 反：freedom, flexibility.
**English explanation:** A limit or condition that restricts what can be done or achieved.
1. Memory is the main constraint on this device. — 内存是这台设备的主要限制。
2. We designed the study around the time constraint. — 我们围绕时间限制设计了这项研究。

### enforce
/ɪnˈfɔrs/ **v.** 执行、强制实施（规则/限制）。搭配：enforce a rule/limit/policy. 近：implement, uphold; 反：waive, disregard.
**English explanation:** To make sure a rule, law, or limit is obeyed, sometimes by applying penalties.
1. The server enforces a limit of ten requests per second. — 服务器强制执行每秒十个请求的限制。
2. The school enforces its safety policy consistently. — 学校始终如一地执行安全政策。

### threshold
/ˈθrɛʃˌhoʊld/ **n.** 阈值；门槛。搭配：set/cross/exceed a threshold. 近：cutoff, limit; 反：无常用反义词.
**English explanation:** A level or point at which something begins, changes, or qualifies.
1. The alert triggers when latency exceeds the threshold. — 延迟超过阈值时会触发警报。
2. Applicants must meet the minimum threshold. — 申请者必须达到最低门槛。

### allocation
/ˌæləˈkeɪʃən/ **n.** 分配；分配额。搭配：resource/budget/memory allocation. 近：distribution, assignment; 反：withholding.
**English explanation:** The act of assigning or distributing a limited amount of something among people, tasks, or uses.
1. Memory allocation failed during startup. — 启动时内存分配失败。
2. The allocation of funds will be reviewed. — 资金分配将接受审查。

### backlog
/ˈbækˌlɔɡ/ **n.** 积压的工作/请求。搭配：clear/reduce/build up a backlog. 近：queue, accumulated work; 反：empty queue.
**English explanation:** Work or requests that have accumulated because they have not yet been completed.
1. The team is working through a backlog of issues. — 团队正在处理积压的问题。
2. A slow consumer caused the request backlog to grow. — 消费者处理过慢导致请求积压增加。

### throughput
/ˈθruˌpʊt/ **n.** 吞吐量；单位时间内处理的工作量。搭配：increase/measure throughput. 近：processing rate; 反：无常用反义词（常与 latency 对照）.
**English explanation:** The amount of work or data a system processes in a given period.
1. Batching increased throughput by 20%. — 批处理使吞吐量提高了 20%。
2. We measured throughput under load. — 我们测量了负载下的吞吐量。

### latency
/ˈleɪtənsi/ **n.** 延迟；响应所需时间。搭配：reduce/measure/tail latency. 近：delay, response time; 反：无常用反义词（常与 throughput 对照）.
**English explanation:** The time between making a request or taking an action and receiving a response.
1. The update reduced median latency. — 这次更新降低了中位延迟。
2. Tail latency rose during the traffic spike. — 流量激增期间尾延迟上升了。

### contention
/kənˈtɛnʃən/ **n.** 争用、竞争（资源）；争论。搭配：lock/resource contention. 近：competition, conflict; 反：cooperation, uncontended access.
**English explanation:** Competition among processes or users for a shared resource; it can also mean disagreement.
1. Lock contention limited the speedup. — 锁竞争限制了加速效果。
2. There is some contention over the interpretation. — 对这种解释存在一些争议。

### transient
/ˈtrænziənt/ **adj./n.** 短暂的、瞬时的；短暂现象。搭配：transient error/state/response. 近：temporary, brief; 反：persistent, sustained.
**English explanation:** Lasting only briefly or present for a short time.
1. The service experienced a transient failure. — 服务出现了一次短暂故障。
2. Ignore transient spikes when computing the steady-state average. — 计算稳态平均值时忽略瞬时峰值。

### sustained
/səˈsteɪnd/ **adj.** 持续的、长期维持的。搭配：sustained load/growth/effort. 近：continuous, prolonged; 反：brief, intermittent.
**English explanation:** Continuing for a long time or maintained without stopping.
1. The system handled sustained traffic. — 系统承受住了持续流量。
2. Sustained effort improved her writing. — 持续努力提升了她的写作。

### aggregate
/ˈæɡrɪɡət/ **adj./n.; ˈæɡrɪˌɡeɪt/ v.** 总计的；集合体；汇总。搭配：aggregate data/score; aggregate results. 近：combined, total; 反：individual, disaggregate.
**English explanation:** Formed by combining separate parts; as a verb, to collect separate values into a total.
1. The aggregate score hides differences between groups. — 总分掩盖了组间差异。
2. We aggregate measurements across runs. — 我们汇总多次运行的测量结果。

### propagate
/ˈprɑpəˌɡeɪt/ **v.** 传播、传递（错误/变化/信号）。搭配：propagate an update/error/change. 近：spread, transmit; 反：contain, suppress.
**English explanation:** To spread or pass something, such as a signal, change, or error, from one place to another.
1. The configuration change propagated to every node. — 配置更改传播到了每个节点。
2. A small error can propagate through the calculation. — 一个小误差可能在计算过程中逐步传播。

### implicit
/ɪmˈplɪsɪt/ **adj.** 含蓄的、未明说的；隐式的。搭配：implicit assumption/consent/conversion. 近：implied, indirect; 反：explicit, stated.
**English explanation:** Suggested or understood without being stated directly.
1. The argument relies on an implicit assumption. — 这个论证依赖一个未明说的假设。
2. The language performs an implicit conversion. — 这种语言会执行隐式转换。

### explicit
/ɪkˈsplɪsɪt/ **adj.** 明确说出的；显式的。搭配：explicit instruction/consent/type. 近：clear, stated; 反：implicit, tacit.
**English explanation:** Stated clearly and directly, leaving little room for doubt.
1. The protocol requires explicit consent. — 该协议要求明确同意。
2. Add an explicit type to avoid ambiguity. — 加上显式类型以避免歧义。

### empirical
/ɛmˈpɪrɪkəl/ **adj.** 基于观察、实验或数据的。搭配：empirical evidence/study/result. 近：observational, evidence-based; 反：theoretical, speculative.
**English explanation:** Based on observation, experiment, or measured data rather than theory alone.
1. The claim needs empirical support. — 这一主张需要实证支持。
2. We compared the prediction with empirical results. — 我们将预测与实证结果作了比较。

### hypothesis
/haɪˈpɑθəsɪs/ **n.** 假设、待检验的解释。搭配：test/formulate/reject a hypothesis. 近：proposition, conjecture; 反：established fact.
**English explanation:** A proposed explanation or prediction that can be tested with evidence.
1. The experiment tested our hypothesis. — 这项实验检验了我们的假设。
2. We proposed an alternative hypothesis. — 我们提出了一个替代假设。

### infer
/ɪnˈfɜr/ **v.** 推断、推论。搭配：infer X from Y. 近：deduce, conclude; 反：observe directly.
**English explanation:** To reach a conclusion from evidence or reasoning rather than direct observation.
1. We infer the cause from the timing of the failures. — 我们根据故障发生的时间推断原因。
2. You cannot infer intent from this message alone. — 不能仅凭这条消息推断意图。

### attribute
/əˈtrɪbjut/ **v.; ˈætrɪˌbjut/ n.** 把……归因于；属性。搭配：attribute X to Y; user attribute. 近：ascribe, assign; 反：misattribute.
**English explanation:** To say that something was caused by or belongs to a particular source; as a noun, a quality or property.
1. They attributed the gain to better caching. — 他们把性能提升归因于更好的缓存。
2. Age is one attribute recorded in the dataset. — 年龄是数据集中记录的一项属性。

### baseline
/ˈbeɪsˌlaɪn/ **n.** 基线；比较的参照点。搭配：establish/compare against a baseline. 近：reference point, benchmark; 反：无常用反义词.
**English explanation:** A starting level or reference point used for comparison.
1. We compared the new method against a strong baseline. — 我们将新方法与一个有力的基线作比较。
2. The baseline remained unchanged across trials. — 所有试验中的基线保持不变。

### outlier
/ˈaʊtˌlaɪər/ **n.** 离群值；异常个体/情况。搭配：identify/remove an outlier. 近：anomaly, extreme value; 反：typical value.
**English explanation:** A value or case that is unusually far from the others in a set.
1. One outlier shifted the average considerably. — 一个离群值显著拉动了平均数。
2. We inspected each outlier before excluding it. — 排除前我们检查了每个离群值。

### heuristic
/hjʊˈrɪstɪk/ **n./adj.** 启发式方法；基于经验规则的。搭配：useful/simple heuristic. 近：rule of thumb, approximation; 反：exact method.
**English explanation:** A practical rule or method that helps find an answer quickly but does not guarantee an exact result.
1. The heuristic works well on typical inputs. — 这个启发式方法对典型输入效果不错。
2. We use a simple heuristic to select candidates. — 我们用一个简单的经验规则筛选候选项。

### criterion
/kraɪˈtɪriən/ **n.** 标准、判据；复数 criteria。搭配：meet/satisfy a criterion. 近：standard, measure; 反：无常用反义词.
**English explanation:** A standard used to judge or decide something; its plural is criteria.
1. Accuracy was the primary criterion. — 准确度是首要标准。
2. Each criterion was defined before analysis. — 每项判据都在分析前确定。

### fallback
/ˈfɔlˌbæk/ **n.** 备用方案；后备选项。搭配：fallback option/plan/mechanism. 近：backup, alternative; 反：primary option.
**English explanation:** A backup option used when the preferred plan or system is unavailable or fails.
1. The local cache is our fallback. — 本地缓存是我们的备用方案。
2. Add a fallback for unavailable models. — 为不可用的模型添加后备方案。

### backpressure
/ˈbækˌprɛʃər/ **n.** 背压；下游变慢时向上游传递的流量控制信号。搭配：apply/propagate backpressure. 近：flow control; 反：unbounded buffering.
**English explanation:** A signal that slows upstream input when a downstream component cannot process work quickly enough.
1. Backpressure prevents the queue from growing without limit. — 背压可防止队列无限增长。
2. The consumer sends backpressure when it falls behind. — 消费者落后时会发出背压信号。

### outage
/ˈaʊtɪdʒ/ **n.** 服务中断、停电。搭配：service/network/power outage. 近：interruption, downtime; 反：uptime, availability.
**English explanation:** A period when a service, network, or power supply is unavailable.
1. The outage affected users in three regions. — 这次服务中断影响了三个地区的用户。
2. A power outage shut down the lab. — 停电导致实验室关闭。

### robustness
/roʊˈbʌstnəs/ **n.** 稳健性、抗干扰能力。搭配：robustness to noise/change. 近：resilience, reliability; 反：fragility.
**English explanation:** The ability to keep working or produce reliable results despite errors, noise, or changing conditions.
1. We evaluated the model's robustness to noise. — 我们评估了模型对噪声的稳健性。
2. Robustness matters more than peak speed here. — 在这里，稳健性比峰值速度更重要。

### scalability
/ˌskeɪləˈbɪləti/ **n.** 可扩展性；规模增大时维持性能的能力。搭配：horizontal/vertical scalability. 近：capacity to scale; 反：limited capacity.
**English explanation:** The ability of a system or process to handle growth in users, data, or workload.
1. The design improves horizontal scalability. — 该设计提升了水平扩展能力。
2. We tested scalability as the dataset grew. — 随着数据集增大，我们测试了可扩展性。

### deployment
/dɪˈplɔɪmənt/ **n.** 部署；部署版本/过程。搭配：production/gradual deployment. 近：rollout, installation; 反：rollback, removal.
**English explanation:** The act of putting software, equipment, or a plan into use; it can also mean the version or arrangement put into service.
1. The deployment completed without errors. — 部署顺利完成，没有错误。
2. We delayed deployment until the checks passed. — 我们等检查通过后才部署。

### workload
/ˈwɜrkˌloʊd/ **n.** 工作负载；需要处理的任务量。搭配：representative/heavy workload. 近：task load, demand; 反：idle state.
**English explanation:** The amount and type of work that a person or system must handle.
1. The benchmark uses a representative workload. — 该基准测试使用了有代表性的工作负载。
2. The workload shifted toward short requests. — 工作负载转向了短请求。

### benchmark
/ˈbɛntʃˌmɑrk/ **n./v.** 基准；基准测试/比较。搭配：benchmark against; performance benchmark. 近：test, measure; 反：无常用反义词.
**English explanation:** A reference point for comparison; as a verb, to measure performance against a standard or another system.
1. We benchmarked the new storage engine. — 我们对新的存储引擎进行了基准测试。
2. This benchmark reflects real application behavior. — 这个基准测试反映了真实应用行为。

### mitigate
/ˈmɪtəˌɡeɪt/ **v.** 减轻、缓解（影响或风险）。搭配：mitigate risk/impact/harm. 近：reduce, alleviate; 反：aggravate, worsen.
**English explanation:** To make a harmful effect, risk, or problem less severe.
1. Replication mitigates the risk of data loss. — 副本机制降低了数据丢失风险。
2. The policy is intended to mitigate harm. — 这项政策旨在减轻伤害。

### validate
/ˈvæləˌdeɪt/ **v.** 验证有效性/正确性；确认。搭配：validate a model/result/input. 近：verify, confirm; 反：invalidate.
**English explanation:** To check that something is correct, effective, or supported by evidence.
1. We validated the result on a second dataset. — 我们在第二个数据集上验证了结果。
2. The server validates every request. — 服务器会验证每个请求。

### uncertainty
/ʌnˈsɜrtənti/ **n.** 不确定性；未知程度。搭配：quantify/acknowledge uncertainty. 近：doubt, ambiguity; 反：certainty.
**English explanation:** A lack of complete knowledge or confidence about what is true or what will happen.
1. The estimate has substantial uncertainty. — 这个估算存在相当大的不确定性。
