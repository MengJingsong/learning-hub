# Academic English Explanations

Each expression includes a Chinese explanation, a related expression, and two contextual examples with translations. Claims are phrased cautiously where evidence is limited.

## Core

### With respect to
**意思：** 关于/就……而言，正式语体。类似：*regarding; in terms of.*
1. With respect to latency, the two systems performed similarly. — 就延迟而言，两个系统表现相似。
2. We found no difference with respect to age. — 关于年龄，我们没有发现差异。

### rule out
**意思：** 排除某种可能性。类似：*exclude; eliminate.*
1. These results rule out a simple explanation. — 这些结果排除了一个简单解释。
2. We cannot rule out measurement error. — 我们不能排除测量误差。

### pin down
**意思：** 准确确定或找出。类似：*identify precisely; establish.*
1. The follow-up study pinned down the source of bias. — 后续研究准确找到了偏差来源。
2. We have not pinned down the mechanism. — 我们还没有确定其机制。

### tease apart
**意思：** 仔细区分纠缠在一起的因素。类似：*disentangle; distinguish.*
1. The design helps tease apart the effects of age and experience. — 该设计有助于区分年龄和经验的影响。
2. More data are needed to tease these explanations apart. — 需要更多数据才能区分这些解释。

### account for
**意思：** 解释、说明；占据一定比例。类似：*explain; make up.*
1. Prior work accounts for the difference in outcomes. — 以往研究解释了结果差异。
2. These cases account for 12% of the sample. — 这些案例占样本的 12%。

### build on
**意思：** 以既有工作为基础继续发展。类似：*extend; draw on.*
1. Our method builds on a standard estimator. — 我们的方法建立在一种标准估计量之上。
2. The study builds on earlier findings. — 这项研究在早期发现的基础上继续推进。

### stem from
**意思：** 起源于、由……导致。类似：*arise from; result from.*
1. The discrepancy stems from different inclusion criteria. — 差异源于不同的纳入标准。
2. Most uncertainty stems from the small sample. — 大部分不确定性来自样本量较小。

### this suggests that
**意思：** 这表明/提示……；谨慎表达推论，不等于证明。类似：*this indicates/implies that*（强度不同）.
1. This suggests that the effect may depend on context. — 这提示该效应可能取决于语境。
2. This suggests that further analysis is warranted. — 这表明有必要做进一步分析。

### rationale
**词汇补充：** /ˌræʃəˈnæl/；常搭配 *rationale for/behind a choice, provide a rationale*；近义 *reasoning, justification*；反义 *無常用反义词*。
**意思：** 理由、理论依据或设计动机。类似：*reasoning; justification.*
1. The rationale for the control condition is explained below. — 下文解释了设置对照条件的理由。
2. We clarified the rationale behind the measure. — 我们阐明了采用该测量方法的依据。

### intuition
**词汇补充：** /ˌɪntuˈɪʃən/；常搭配 *develop an intuition, mathematical intuition*；近义 *instinct, insight*；反义 *formal analysis*（概念对照）。
**意思：** 直觉；对问题的非正式理解。类似：*instinct; informal understanding.*
1. The intuition is that caching avoids repeated work. — 直观理解是缓存可以避免重复工作。
2. Her intuition about the trend was correct. — 她对趋势的直觉判断是正确的。

### the key thing to notice
**意思：** 值得特别注意的关键点。类似：*the main point to note.*
1. The key thing to notice is that variance also increased. — 关键要注意的是方差也增加了。
2. The key thing to notice is the change in the control group. — 关键是观察对照组的变化。

### main takeaway
**意思：** 最重要的结论/收获。类似：*key finding; bottom line.*
1. The main takeaway is that the effect is modest. — 最重要的结论是效应幅度不大。
2. Our main takeaway is that the approach is feasible. — 我们最重要的结论是该方法可行。

### shed light on
**意思：** 帮助解释、使问题更清楚。类似：*clarify; illuminate.*
1. The interviews shed light on why users left. — 访谈有助于解释用户离开的原因。
2. This analysis sheds light on the source of the variance. — 这项分析让方差来源更清楚。

### give rise to
**意思：** 引起、导致；较正式。类似：*cause; lead to.*
1. The policy may give rise to unintended effects. — 这项政策可能引发意料之外的影响。
2. Correlated predictors can give rise to unstable estimates. — 相关预测变量可能导致估计不稳定。

### generalize to
**意思：** 推广适用于另一群体/情境。类似：*extend to; apply to.*
1. The result may not generalize to older adults. — 这个结果可能不能推广到老年人群。
2. We tested whether the model generalizes to new domains. — 我们测试模型能否推广到新领域。

### be consistent with
**意思：** 与证据/解释相符，但不表示证明。类似：*agree with; align with.*
1. The pattern is consistent with the proposed mechanism. — 这种模式与提出的机制相符。
2. Her account is consistent with the recorded timeline. — 她的叙述与记录的时间线一致。

### be driven by
**意思：** 主要由……推动/导致。类似：*be caused primarily by; be attributable to.*
1. The increase was driven by two large sites. — 增长主要由两个大型站点带动。
2. The result is driven by a small subset of cases. — 结果主要由一小部分案例决定。

### confirm / support / be consistent with / refute
**意思：** confirm=确认；support=支持但未必证明；be consistent with=与……相符；refute=反驳。类似：*corroborate; lend support; contradict.*
1. The replication supports the original finding. — 复现实验支持了原发现。
2. The new evidence refutes the initial explanation. — 新证据驳斥了最初的解释。

### observe / infer / predict
**意思：** observe=直接观察；infer=根据证据推断；predict=预测尚未观察的结果。类似：*measure; conclude; forecast.*
1. We observed a delay and inferred queue saturation. — 我们观察到延迟，并推断队列已饱和。
2. The model predicts outcomes that we can later observe. — 模型预测了我们之后可以观察到的结果。

### evidence / finding / result
**意思：** evidence=证据总称；finding=研究发现；result=分析/实验结果。类似：*data; observation; outcome.*
1. The evidence is consistent across three studies. — 三项研究中的证据一致。
2. The main finding was a small but reliable gain. — 主要发现是提升幅度虽小但稳定。

### attribute X to Y / result in / result from
**意思：** attribute X to Y=把 X 归因于 Y；result in=导致；result from=由……导致。类似：*ascribe to; lead to; arise from.*
1. We attribute the gain to better sampling. — 我们把提升归因于更好的抽样。
2. The change resulted in lower variance; the reduction resulted from a larger sample. — 这项改动降低了方差；这种降低源自更大的样本。

### interpret with caution
**意思：** 谨慎解读，不超出证据强度。类似：*treat cautiously; avoid overinterpreting.*
1. The subgroup result should be interpreted with caution. — 子组结果应谨慎解读。
2. Interpret the estimate with caution because the interval is wide. — 由于区间很宽，应谨慎解读该估算。

### be limited to X
**意思：** 适用范围仅限于 X。类似：*be restricted to; apply only to.*
1. The analysis is limited to English-language publications. — 分析仅限英文出版物。
2. These conclusions are limited to the tested workload. — 这些结论仅适用于测试过的工作负载。

### raise the possibility of X
**意思：** 提出 X 可能存在，但尚未证实。类似：*suggest that X may be possible.*
1. The pattern raises the possibility of selection bias. — 这种模式提出了存在选择偏差的可能性。
2. These findings raise the possibility of a delayed effect. — 这些发现提示可能存在延迟效应。

### support the hypothesis that X
**意思：** 提供支持假设 X 的证据，但不必然证明它。类似：*lend support to the hypothesis that X.*
1. The results support the hypothesis that practice improves recall. — 结果支持练习能改善回忆这一假设。
2. The data support the hypothesis that load affects tail latency. — 数据支持负载会影响尾延迟这一假设。

### consistent with prior work
**意思：** 与先前研究结果相符。类似：*in agreement with previous studies.*
1. Our estimate is consistent with prior work. — 我们的估算与以往研究一致。
2. This pattern is consistent with prior work on memory. — 这种模式与以往关于记忆的研究相符。

### take X into account
**意思：** 把 X 纳入考虑。类似：*consider; account for.*
1. The model takes age into account. — 模型考虑了年龄因素。
2. We took measurement error into account. — 我们把测量误差考虑在内。

### future work
**意思：** 未来研究/后续工作。类似：*subsequent research; further work.*
1. Future work should test a broader population. — 未来研究应测试更广泛的人群。
2. We leave this comparison to future work. — 我们将这一比较留待后续工作。

### open question
**意思：** 尚未解决的问题。类似：*unresolved issue; unanswered question.*
1. Whether the effect persists is an open question. — 这种效应是否持续仍是个未解问题。
2. The best sampling strategy remains an open question. — 最佳抽样策略仍未确定。

### statistically significant
**意思：** 按设定统计检验达到显著标准；不等于效果大或重要。类似：*significant at the chosen statistical threshold.*
1. The difference was statistically significant but small. — 差异具有统计显著性，但幅度很小。
2. The result was not statistically significant. — 结果未达到统计显著标准。

### no evidence of X
**意思：** 没有发现支持 X 的证据；不等于证明 X 不存在。类似：*no evidence that X; evidence was not observed for X.*
1. We found no evidence of a treatment effect. — 我们没有发现处理效应的证据。
2. There was no evidence of data leakage. — 没有发现数据泄漏的证据。

### further analysis
**意思：** 进一步分析。类似：*additional examination; follow-up analysis.*
1. Further analysis revealed a subgroup difference. — 进一步分析发现了子组差异。
2. The result warrants further analysis. — 这一结果值得进一步分析。

### prior work
**意思：** 先前研究/已有工作。类似：*previous research; earlier studies.*
1. Prior work used a smaller dataset. — 先前研究使用了较小的数据集。
2. We build on prior work in this area. — 我们在该领域已有工作的基础上继续研究。

### existing literature
**意思：** 现有文献、已发表研究。类似：*published research; current body of work.*
1. The existing literature offers mixed conclusions. — 现有文献得出的结论不一。
2. We reviewed the existing literature before designing the study. — 设计研究前我们回顾了现有文献。

### tentative evidence
**意思：** 初步的、尚不确定的证据。类似：*preliminary evidence; suggestive evidence.*
1. We found tentative evidence of improvement. — 我们发现了改进的初步证据。
2. This remains tentative evidence until replicated. — 在得到复现前，这仍只是初步证据。

### under these conditions
**意思：** 在这些特定条件下。类似：*in this setting; given these conditions.*
1. Under these conditions, the estimator is unbiased. — 在这些条件下，该估计量是无偏的。
2. The service remains stable under these conditions. — 在这些条件下，服务保持稳定。

### beyond the scope of X
**意思：** 超出 X 的范围。类似：*outside the scope of X.*
1. Causal identification is beyond the scope of this paper. — 因果识别超出本文范围。
2. The legal analysis is beyond the scope of our review. — 法律分析超出我们的综述范围。

### extrapolate to X
**意思：** 从已有数据推断/外推到 X；需注意假设。类似：*project to; generalize from data to.*
1. We should not extrapolate to populations absent from the sample. — 不应把结果外推到样本未包含的人群。
2. The model extrapolates poorly to longer sequences. — 该模型对更长序列的外推表现不佳。

### account for potential confounders
**意思：** 考虑可能的混杂因素。类似：*control for possible confounding variables.*
1. The regression accounts for potential confounders. — 回归分析考虑了潜在混杂因素。
2. We matched participants to account for potential confounders. — 我们匹配参与者以控制潜在混杂因素。

### draw on X
**意思：** 借鉴/利用 X。类似：*rely on; build on.*
1. The framework draws on cognitive science. — 该框架借鉴了认知科学。
2. We draw on several independent datasets. — 我们利用了几个独立数据集。

## Useful

### line up with
**意思：** 与……一致、吻合。类似：*match; agree with.*
1. The measurements line up with the predicted trend. — 测量结果与预测趋势吻合。
2. Her account lines up with the lab notes. — 她的说法与实验室记录一致。

### cross-check
**词汇补充：** /ˈkrɔsˌtʃɛk/；常搭配 *cross-check data/results against a source*；近义 *verify, double-check*；反义 *take at face value*。
**意思：** 通过另一来源或方法核对。类似：*verify independently; double-check.*
1. We cross-checked the labels against the source files. — 我们对照源文件核验了标签。
2. Cross-check the estimate with a second method. — 用第二种方法核对这个估算。

### what seems to be happening is
**意思：** 谨慎引出对现象的解释。类似：*it appears that; the likely explanation is.*
1. What seems to be happening is that retries are amplifying the load. — 看起来重试正在放大负载。
2. What seems to be happening is a shift in the sample. — 看起来样本发生了变化。

### motivation behind
**意思：** 某做法/决定背后的动机或理由。类似：*rationale for; reason behind.*
1. The motivation behind the control is to isolate the effect. — 设置对照的动机是分离效应。
2. We explain the motivation behind this assumption. — 我们解释了这一假设背后的理由。

### relax an assumption
**意思：** 放宽假设，使其不那么严格。类似：*weaken/loosen an assumption.*
1. The extension relaxes the independence assumption. — 这一扩展放宽了独立性假设。
2. We relax an assumption about uniform access. — 我们放宽了关于均匀访问的假设。

### come into play
**意思：** 开始起作用/变得相关。类似：*become relevant; take effect.*
1. Memory pressure comes into play at larger scales. — 规模增大后内存压力开始显现。
2. Several factors come into play during deployment. — 部署期间有几个因素会发挥作用。

### hinge on
**意思：** 取决于。类似：*depend on; turn on.*
1. The conclusion hinges on the validity of the measure. — 结论取决于测量是否有效。
2. Success hinges on reliable coordination. — 成功取决于协调是否可靠。

### play a role in
**意思：** 在……中发挥作用/有影响。类似：*contribute to; affect.*
1. Temperature may play a role in the observed variation. — 温度可能影响了观察到的变化。
2. Prior experience played a role in participant choices. — 既往经验影响了参与者的选择。

### flag something
**意思：** 标出/提醒注意某事或问题。类似：*highlight; call attention to.*
1. The reviewer flagged a potential confounder. — 审稿人指出了一个潜在混杂因素。
2. We flag missing values before analysis. — 分析前我们会标记缺失值。

### point out
**意思：** 指出某个事实/问题。类似：*note; draw attention to.*
1. The authors point out a limitation in the sample. — 作者指出样本存在一项局限。
2. She pointed out that the groups were not balanced. — 她指出各组并不均衡。

### hold true
**意思：** 仍然成立/属实。类似：*remain valid; be true.*
1. The relationship holds true across both datasets. — 这种关系在两个数据集中都成立。
2. This assumption may not hold true in practice. — 这个假设在实际中可能不成立。

### carry over to
**意思：** 延续并适用于另一情形。类似：*transfer to; generalize to.*
1. The improvement carried over to the larger model. — 这项改进也适用于更大的模型。
2. The classroom finding may not carry over to workplaces. — 课堂研究发现未必适用于工作场所。

### null result
**意思：** 未发现预期效应/差异的结果，不等于证明绝对没有效应。类似：*no-effect finding; non-significant result.*
1. The study reported a null result for the primary outcome. — 研究报告称主要结果未发现效应。
2. We discuss possible explanations for the null result. — 我们讨论了这一零结果的可能解释。

### positive control
**意思：** 已知会产生预期效应的对照，用于确认实验系统有效。类似：*known-response control.*
1. The positive control confirmed that the assay was working. — 阳性对照确认了检测方法正常工作。
2. We included a positive control in every batch. — 我们在每个批次中都加入了阳性对照。

### preregister a study
**意思：** 在收集/分析数据前预先登记研究问题与方法。类似：*register a protocol in advance.*
1. The team preregistered the study and its outcomes. — 团队预先登记了研究及其结果指标。
2. We preregistered the analysis plan before examining the data. — 查看数据前我们预先登记了分析计划。

### signal-to-noise ratio
**意思：** 信号与噪声之比；也引申为有效信息相对干扰的比例。类似：*signal relative to background noise.*
1. Averaging improved the signal-to-noise ratio. — 求平均提高了信噪比。
2. The long report has a low signal-to-noise ratio. — 这份长报告的有效信息比例很低。

### intervention
**词汇补充：** /ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən/；常搭配 *policy/clinical intervention, intervention group*；近义 *treatment, measure*（研究语境）；反义无固定反义词。
**意思：** 干预措施；研究者施加的处理。类似：*treatment; program/action.*
1. The intervention reduced missed appointments. — 这项干预减少了预约未到的情况。
2. We compared outcomes before and after the intervention. — 我们比较了干预前后的结果。

## Specialized

### Ad hoc
**意思：** 为特定目的临时制定的；有时暗示缺乏通用设计。类似：*purpose-specific; improvised.*
1. We wrote an ad hoc script to inspect the files. — 我们临时写了个脚本检查这些文件。
2. The paper uses an ad hoc definition of success. — 论文为成功临时采用了一个定义。

### Post hoc
**意思：** 事后进行的；统计中指观察数据后才提出/选择的分析。类似：*after the fact; retrospective.*
1. The subgroup analysis was post hoc. — 子组分析是事后进行的。
2. Post hoc explanations should be treated cautiously. — 应谨慎看待事后解释。

### Case-specific
**词汇补充：** /ˈkeɪs spəˌsɪfɪk/；常搭配 *case-specific explanation/rule*；近义 *particular, context-specific*；反义 *general, universal*。
**意思：** 针对特定案例的，不一定能普遍推广。类似：*particular to this case; context-specific.*
1. The recommendation is case-specific. — 这个建议针对这一具体案例。
2. We added a case-specific exception. — 我们增加了一个针对特定案例的例外。

### De facto
**意思：** 实际上的、事实上形成的（不一定有正式规定）。类似：*in practice; in effect.*
1. The library became the de facto standard. — 这个库事实上成了标准。
2. English is the de facto working language in the lab. — 英语事实上是实验室的工作语言。

### In Prose
**意思：** 用连续文字叙述，而非列表、公式或图表。类似：*in running text; in paragraph form.*
1. State the assumption in prose before presenting the equation. — 给出公式前先用文字说明假设。
2. The result is summarized in prose below the table. — 表格下方用文字总结了结果。

### constraint / limit / threshold / ceiling
**意思：** constraint=限制因素；limit=边界/限额；threshold=触发判断的阈值；ceiling=最高上限。类似：*restriction; cap; cutoff.*
1. Memory is the main constraint, and the request limit is fixed. — 内存是主要限制，请求限额是固定的。
2. Once latency crosses the threshold, the ceiling on retries applies. — 延迟超过阈值后，重试次数上限开始适用。

### throughput / latency / capacity
**意思：** throughput=吞吐量；latency=延迟；capacity=可处理的最大量。类似：*processing rate; response time; maximum load.*
1. Batching raised throughput but also increased latency. — 批处理提高了吞吐量，但也增加了延迟。
2. We tested capacity while tracking response time. — 我们在测试容量的同时跟踪响应时间。

### transient / sustained / cumulative / aggregate
**意思：** transient=短暂的；sustained=持续的；cumulative=逐步累积的；aggregate=汇总的/总计的。类似：*temporary; ongoing; accumulated; combined.*
1. We separated transient spikes from sustained load. — 我们区分了瞬时峰值和持续负载。
2. The aggregate score reflects cumulative performance. — 汇总分数反映了累积表现。

### block / defer / reject
**意思：** block=阻止；defer=推迟；reject=拒绝/驳回。类似：*prevent; postpone; decline.*
1. The filter blocks invalid input but defers uncertain cases. — 过滤器阻止无效输入，但推迟处理不确定案例。
2. The committee rejected the proposal. — 委员会驳回了提案。

### fall back / fallback / fall through
**意思：** fall back=退回备用方案；fallback=备用方案（名词/形容词）；fall through=未匹配后继续进入下一选项。类似：*revert; backup; pass to default.*
1. The client falls back to a cached response. — 客户端退回到缓存响应。
2. The unmatched route falls through to the default handler. — 未匹配的路由会进入默认处理程序。

### on its own / in its own / by itself
**意思：** on its own=独自/自动；in its own=必须接名词，意为“在它自己的……”；by itself=本身/单独地。类似：*independently; individually.*
1. The service recovered on its own. — 服务自行恢复了。
2. The result by itself is not conclusive. — 这个结果单独来看并不能下结论。

### while / whereas / unless / until
**意思：** while=当……时/而；whereas=然而、而（对比）；unless=除非；until=直到。类似：*when; in contrast; if not; up to the time.*
1. While the first method is fast, the second is more stable. — 第一种方法快，而第二种更稳定。
2. Wait until the data arrive unless the process fails. — 除非进程失败，否则等数据到达。

### bound / bounded / boundary
**意思：** bound=界限/受限；bounded=有界的；boundary=边界/分界线。类似：*limit; finite; border.*
1. The error is bounded by the measurement precision. — 误差受测量精度限制。
2. We defined a boundary between training and test data. — 我们划定了训练数据与测试数据的边界。

### challenge the notion that X
**意思：** 对 X 这一观念提出质疑。类似：*question the assumption that X.*
1. The findings challenge the notion that age alone predicts performance. — 研究发现质疑了年龄本身能够预测表现这一观念。
2. This result challenges the notion that the process is deterministic. — 这个结果对该过程是确定性的这一看法提出了质疑。

### remain unanswered
**意思：** 仍未得到回答/解决。类似：*remain unresolved; still be unknown.*
1. Several questions remain unanswered. — 仍有几个问题没有答案。
2. The mechanism remains unanswered in the current study. — 当前研究尚未解释其机制。

### corroborate previous findings
**意思：** 为先前发现提供进一步佐证。类似：*confirm earlier results; provide additional support.*
1. The replication corroborates previous findings. — 复现实验佐证了先前发现。
2. Our data corroborate previous findings on sleep and memory. — 我们的数据佐证了先前关于睡眠与记忆的发现。
