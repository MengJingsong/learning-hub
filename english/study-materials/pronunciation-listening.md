## Core
- going to → gonna (casual speech; listening recognition)
- want to → wanna (casual speech; listening recognition)
- flap T between vowels in American English
- consonant-to-vowel linking across word boundaries
- unstressed function words often reduce toward schwa
- sentence stress highlights important content words
- link words smoothly within a thought group
- and → /ən/ or /n/ in casual speech
- to → /tə/ when unstressed
- can → /kən/ when unstressed
