# Study Inbox

Temporary queue for new English items discovered during lookup sessions.

Items here are **not yet part of the formal study corpus** and should not be used for normal review/testing until curated.

## Format

- Use one item per line.
- Prefer a lightweight type tag when obvious:
  - `[vocab]`
  - `[phrase]`
  - `[pattern]`
  - `[pronunciation]`
- Use standard English casing.
- Do not add meanings, examples, or long explanations here.
- Avoid duplicates when possible.
