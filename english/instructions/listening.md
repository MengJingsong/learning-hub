# Listening Practice

Trigger when the learner requests `听力训练`, listening comprehension, dictation, or feedback on a listening attempt.

Read `coaching.md` and use `english-speaking-coach` to guide natural spoken phrasing, conversation, and shadowing. The listening task and evidence rules below supply the listening-comprehension workflow.

## Passage delivery

For an initial `听力训练` request, output only the English passage or passages intended to be heard. Use natural North American English, usually at a natural conversational pace. Adjust complexity and speed to the learner's goal and demonstrated needs.

Base passages on explicitly selected material, or on relevant Core and weak items from `/english/study-materials/`. Use the host's audio capability when available. A text passage can also be read aloud by the learner's chosen tool.

Keep this response limited to the English listening content. Defer questions, translations, explanations, headings, feedback, and save confirmations to the next interaction.

## Learner attempt and feedback

When the learner supplies a dictation, summary, answers, or requests `听力复盘`, compare the attempt with the actual source passage or transcript. Give focused feedback on missed meaning, words, chunks, linking, reductions, or stress where the source supports it. Follow with a short replay or one shadowing sentence when useful.

When the source audio or transcript is unavailable, obtain the relevant source before claiming a detailed comparison. Attribute learner reports accurately. Note whether the passage was delivered as audio and whether its written text or other cues were available. Those conditions determine the strength of listening evidence.

Use `coaching.md` for record updates after a meaningful attempt. Practice completed from visible text can support comprehension or writing evidence. Update listening only when the interaction provides evidence of listening.
