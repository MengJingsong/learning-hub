# Words, Phrases, and Grammar

Trigger when the learner asks to learn, practice, review, or test English words, phrases, collocations, or grammar. Examples include `学习这些短语`, `练习冠词`, and `复习我容易忘的单词`.

Read `coaching.md` and use `english-learning-coach` for this task. For a review or test, also follow `review.md` or `test.md`. Speaking and listening objectives follow their corresponding task instructions.

Read `/english/english-study/learning-plan.md` for the current learning preference and walkthrough position. Use the learner's selected material or choose relevant formal items using the shared selection rules. Reuse known context and start with the requested learning activity.

Follow the plan's three stages: provide the English passage, wait for and retain the learner's Chinese translation, then on `继续` give the documented explanation and item-by-item translation feedback before moving to the next batch. In that explanation pass, open each exact-heading companion note in `/english/study-materials/explanations/` and include its documented teaching. For lexical points, consult Oxford Advanced American Dictionary and Cambridge Dictionary to inform the explanation. Give a concise English explanation alongside the Chinese meaning, plus relevant pronunciation, register, collocations, usage, and examples. No source links are needed. Evaluate only comprehension evidenced by the learner's translation; update only that mastery dimension under the shared record rules. Follow the plan's batch and corpus order.

Use retrieval questions, learner production, quizzes, and retry exercises when the learner explicitly asks for practice or assessment. For such requests, wait for an attempt before feedback and hide answers when appropriate. The learner's requested teaching format takes priority over a skill's default practice loop.

Use available word-explanation, synonym-comparison, or writing-feedback skills when they add value to a specific part of the lesson. Keep one coherent exercise and feedback flow.

Keep detailed explanations in the conversation. Follow `coaching.md` for learning records and new inbox candidates.
