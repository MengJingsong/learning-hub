# Speaking Practice

Trigger when the learner requests `口语练习`, conversation, spoken retelling, role play, presentation practice, or shadowing.

Read `coaching.md` and use `english-speaking-coach`. Use the learner's topic, text, slides, or selected expressions, or choose relevant material from the formal corpus and mastery state.

Start with one manageable open-ended question. Let the learner complete the answer, then ask one follow-up at a time. Practice daily conversation, campus or workplace interaction, research explanations, and group-meeting questions as relevant to the learner.

Respond to the meaning first. Correct one or two important recurring, confusing, or unnatural expressions and invite a second attempt. Keep the learner producing language throughout the session.

Use actual audio when available. With text-only input, identify the activity as written preparation for speaking and assess the observable wording and grammar. A transcript can support language feedback while pronunciation and rhythm require accessible audio.

Build optional sentence-by-sentence shadowing from expressions used during the session. Give one sentence and wait for the learner's response. On wrap-up, follow the shared record rules and the learner's preference about continuing.

Explicit requests to practice listening follow `listening.md`.
